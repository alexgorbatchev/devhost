# Watch and restart services from the toolbar: proposed experience

Diagrams: [current](../services-panel-current.html), [proposed](../services-panel-proposed.html). [Wireframe](../wireframes/services-panel.html).

Status: design proposal, not implemented behavior. The [shared UX pattern reference](../patterns.md) wins where this proposal disagrees with it.

Source revision: alexgorbatchev/devhost `edb5c0fc01cabae490e92ac23d4c785ec896c4ed`. Features: TB-03, SVC-01, SVC-02, SVC-03, SVC-04, SVC-05. Redesign ticket: tracked privately.

Product rule: `stacks/recovery.md` states the recovery contract: "a service that crashes, times out, or returns errors does not take the stack down with it. Its hostname shows what went wrong, with recent logs and a restart button, and the other services keep running." The owner's rules add that external services (`managed = false`) show status only and that every manifest setting applies through hot reload. So one failed service leaving the others running is intended, an external service offering no **Restart** or **Start** is intended, and a rejected manifest edit leaving the stack on its accepted manifest is intended; none of them is a defect. The gap is around them: the toolbar cannot tell starting, failed, killed, not started and disconnected apart, no restart says what it restarted or how it ended, the one stack-wide restart sits beside the everyday one without a second step, and Restart Stack's rollback breaks the recovery contract when one service cannot start (`error-pages-01`, fixed first by its stopgap).

## Target experience

The person glances at the bar: the Services trigger counts every service of the stack and leads with what needs them ("3 of 6 running · 1 failed · 2 not started"), as one dot per service while the dots fit on the bar's row and as a fixed-width summary when they would not. Opening it keeps the page, its scroll position and the person's place: an anchored panel lists every service in its group, each row with its state word, cause and time, its route as text, and its actions; focus lands in the filter when the rows overflow, else on the first row action. Pressing a row's **Restart** keeps focus on that button, which reads "Restarting api…" while the row steps through stopping, starting and "health check 2 s of 30 s"; the row then becomes the receipt, "Restarted api · 2.6 s", announced once. **Restart 2 Changed**, or **Restart docs** when nothing changed, names its set, and Alt+Ctrl+R restarts the same set from any page, reporting through an activity line and an outcome notice when the panel is closed. Not-started services sit in their groups with **Start**, and **Start 2 Services** starts every one of them; starts and restarts of other services queue on their rows instead of locking each other. **Restart Stack**, set apart at the far end of the footer, expands in place into "Restart all 6 services with new automatic ports? Took 14 s last time." with **Cancel** focused; once confirmed, its button stays as the busy control, every row shows its step, and the panel ends with one outcome that counts what runs. A failure stays on its row with the exit, the signal, the last output lines, the probe and what devhost ran, led by the action that can fix it, until the person dismisses it or the same restart succeeds. A lost connection draws every row as unknown with the time it was last seen, never down, and makes every write unavailable with the reason. Restarts cannot be taken back, so there is no Undo: every outcome says what changed, and a failed Restart Stack restores what ran before, service by service. Benchmarks: Docker Desktop shows a partly started Compose project as "Running (3/6)" with **Start** on each stopped container and words such as "Exited (1)" for each state; Tilt keeps each resource's own state, the elapsed time and a pending update after a file change, and its trigger names the resource it updates; JetBrains' Services window filters and groups run configurations and asks "Stop and Rerun" before replacing a run; VS Code keeps a busy control focused, caps visible notifications at three and shows "Reconnecting…" with the last known state instead of an outage; Kubernetes keeps a pod Running but not Ready until its probe passes, marks pods Unknown rather than Failed when it loses their node, and reports a rollback as an outcome of its own; `docker compose up --wait` certifies readiness, not a step. Competitor behavior is evidence here, never a requirement.

## Target steps

Today: 5 steps, the last a guess: trigger, panel, scan bare names for the row, **Restart** (focus falls to the page), watch a dot turn green with no outcome. Proposed: 4 steps: trigger, panel with focus where the work is, **Restart** on the row, outcome on that row. The shortcut path stays 2 steps, but ends in a stated outcome instead of one 8 px dot spinning: press, read the activity line and its outcome.

1. **Read the stack at a glance (the published service state, read only).** The Services trigger shows one dot per service of the stack, the services the run left out included as hollow dots, while the dots fit on the bar's single row at the current window width; when they would wrap the bar, it shows a fixed-width summary instead, "3/6 · 1 failed: api · 2 not started", naming up to three services that need the person, then "+N" (`BAR-1`, `SURF-10`, `ACT-6`, `ACT-13`, `WORD-5`). Each dot's shape and color tell its state, the same in forced colors (`ACT-13`). Its accessible name is the summary spelled out: "Services: 3 of 6 running, 1 failed: api, 2 not started, 1 changed". The changed badge, "1 changed", and its restart icon sit beside the summary and stay there beside a failure (`SEL-2`, `ACT-8`). A service that is starting counts as starting, "0 of 2 running · 2 starting", never as failed (`ACT-4`). Every dot, row and state word this flow draws comes from one shared service state indicator (shape, state word and accessible name) and one shared module of state words, labels and sentence builders under `packages/devhost-ui/src/`, both shipped in the devtools Shadow DOM stylesheet and in the status pages' document stylesheet, so startup-and-recovery's crash view and recovery pages and stack-directory's cards draw each state with the same shape and words (`SURF-14`, `ACT-13`, `WORD-6`). When the toolbar is collapsed, toolbar-shell's badge reads the same model: it takes the look of the highest condition in `ACT-7`'s order and names what needs the person, "1 failed · 1 changed", with api's failure notice in one line beside it (`SURF-8`, `ACT-7`); a routine restart, of one row or of the whole stack, never raises it, because a transition is never a failure (`ACT-7`, `ACT-3`).
   - Defaults: dots while they fit, decided by the fit test at every viewport, never by a service count (`SURF-10`).
   - Keys: Alt+Ctrl+B focuses the bar; the trigger is an arrow stop (ArrowLeft, ArrowRight, Home, End); Enter, Space or the arrow that points toward the panel opens it (`KEY-3`, `KEY-4`).
2. **Open the Services panel (`/__devhost__/ws/health`, read only).** An anchored popover (`SURF-2`) opens with the stack's summary sentence at the top, "Acme Shop · 3 of 6 running · 1 failed · 2 not started"; it, the trigger and every other open tab of the stack update from the one health stream without a refresh (`ACT-12`). Rows are grouped by repository when the stack has more than one, then by the manifest's group or include file, each header with its count and its state ("packages/ · 10 · all running"); a heading labels two or more rows, and no heading repeats the panel's own title (`MENU-2`). When the rows overflow the panel's height, the panel gains a filter field, focused on open, that matches name, hostname and group as the person types and keeps group headers with their counts (`SEL-10`, `SURF-10`), and the rows that need the person (failed, exited, blocked, changed) are pinned in a group above the rest; the footer actions stay outside the scrolling area at every length (`SURF-10`, `SURF-9`). The panel is placed and sized so it never overlaps the notice stack, in either corner placement and with chat docked compact or expanded, and paints above docked chat, so a failure notice beside it stays readable and clickable while it is open (`SURF-1`, `SURF-9`).
   - A row: the state shape, the name, the state word with its cause and time whenever the service is not plainly running ("exited with code 1 at 14:02", "starting · health check 4 s of 60 s", "not started · left out of this run", "queued behind Restart mail", "blocked"), the route as text (host and path, a long hostname shortened in the middle with the full URL in its tooltip and accessible name), the port or "external", and a "changed" tag that names its files (`ACT-2`, `ACT-8`, `WORD-6`, `WORD-8`); the shape, the state word and the row's accessible name come from the shared state indicator and words module (step 1, `SURF-14`). When the page saw its own service's dev server apply an edit in place, the page adds its note beside the tag on that row and in the changed badge's tooltip and accessible description, "applied in place by Vite's hot update", from reload-control's detection, and the published mark stays (`ACT-8`). The name links only to an address that answers: the page route, else the service's health URL, and opens it in the same tab, as every devhost link does except Watch Live Activity and **Open in Editor** (today it opens a new tab); a path route with neither shows its route as copyable text (`SURF-15`).
   - Row actions, each a Tab stop in row order whose accessible name names its target ("Restart api"): **Restart** on a managed service; **Start** on a not-started one; **Logs**, which opens the pinned log panel filtered to that service (`SURF-13`); and **Edit** on a failed or exited one (`MENU-4`) (`KEY-10`, `WORD-2`). **Restart** and **Start** each have the same-named terminal command with the same behavior, `devhost service restart api` and `devhost service start admin` (`MENU-6`, `CC-11`).
   - An external service's row names its check, its last result and its time, "reachable · GET search.acme.test/health · checked 14:02" or "not reachable · GET search.acme.test/health · checked 14:02", and "runs outside devhost", with no **Restart**, **Start**, **Logs** or changed tag (`ACT-9`). A row that is not reachable leads with **Check Again** (accessible name "Check Again search"), which asks the services runtime to run that check now (read only, Backend 12) and updates the row's result and time, because only the person can know that the outside service came back (`MENU-5`, `HOLD-1`). The panel says once, as visible text, that devhost checks external services but cannot restart them (`ACT-9`, `MENU-3`).
   - Defaults: every group expanded; with no filter, focus on the first row action (`KEY-6`).
   - Keys: Tab and Shift+Tab move through the filter, each row's actions and the footer in visual order; Tab past the last control or Shift+Tab before the first closes the panel and returns focus to the trigger (`KEY-7`); Escape or a click outside the panel closes it and returns focus to the trigger, or, when the panel was opened with the pointer, to the page element that had focus before (`KEY-5`, `KEY-6`, `KEY-8`).
3. **Restart one service (`POST /__devhost__/restart-service` with an `operationId`, one write).** **Restart** runs on one press, because restarting one named service is not disruptive (`REV-1`). The pressed button keeps focus, `aria-busy` and `aria-disabled`, and reads "Restarting api…" (`KEY-11`, `FB-9`); the row shows each step with the elapsed time, updated at least once a second: queued when another operation runs ahead of it, stopping, starting, "health check 2 s of 30 s", then running (`FB-1`, `ACT-3`, `STATE-1`). Every other row keeps its own state (`ACT-3`). The route answers `202 Accepted` with the operation's record before the restart runs (`ACT-1`, `CC-37`), and the stack's terminal and log print one outcome line (`FB-10`, `FB-7`).
   - The restart succeeds only once api passes its health check, with no leftover of a killed run answering in its place, and its routes are updated (`FB-5`, `ACT-11`); it keeps api's automatic port, as today. When a leftover process of an earlier, killed run of this stack holds api's port or hostname, the restart fails before launching and the row names that process, its PID and port, and the command that stops it, never "Restarted api" (`ACT-11`, `FB-5`, `FAIL-3`); stack-run-stop owns finding leftovers and words that command.
   - A second **Restart** of api while api restarts is unavailable with "api is already restarting" (`REV-12`, `HOLD-3`, `WORD-7`); a **Restart** of another service queues behind it, and so does any **Restart** during a hot reload (`SEL-7`, `HOLD-3`). While the stack starts, shuts down or runs a stack-wide operation, every **Restart** and **Start** is unavailable with that operation and its progress as the reason, "Starting Acme Shop · 2 of 6 started", "Stopping Acme Shop · 3 of 6 stopped" or "Restart Stack is running · 3 of 6 started" (`HOLD-3`, `CC-8`).
4. **Read the outcome on the row, or recover in place (one write per retry).** Success replaces the busy state on the row: "Restarted api · 2.6 s" (`FB-2`, `FB-3`, `WORD-4`), announced once in the polite region (`FB-9`); it stays while the panel is open (`FB-6`). Failure turns the row failed or exited with one cause sentence and its evidence: "api exited with code 1 at 14:02 before its health check", its own last output lines frozen at the exit, the probe it failed ("http://127.0.0.1:55891/ answered 502 after 30 s of 30 s"), or the program that holds a fixed port, and what devhost ran: the command, its folder when it differs from the stack's, and the manifest file and line, "Ran bun run src/server.ts in api/ · devhost.toml line 14" (`FAIL-2`, `FAIL-3`, `WORD-7`, `WORD-8`). Focus stays on **Restart**, which takes the cause sentence as its accessible description, and the failure is announced once in the assertive region (`KEY-11`, `FB-9`). The row leads with `MENU-5`'s action for its cause: **Restart**, with **Logs** and **Edit** beside it. A retry that fails the same way updates the row's report in place instead of adding another, "api exited with code 1 · 2 times, last at 14:05", and **Edit** then leads (`FAIL-4`, `MENU-5`). The failure stays on the row until the person dismisses it with the row's **Dismiss** (accessible name "Dismiss api's failure") or the same restart succeeds; health messages, reconnects and closing the panel never remove it, and a closed panel leaves it as a failure notice (`FB-6`, `FB-2`, `KEY-10`).
   - **Edit** opens the Configuration window at the service's definition, with the exit, the cause and the last lines in its banner (`MENU-4`, `SURF-4`); with the config editor off, the row shows the manifest file and line with **Copy** and **Open in Editor** instead.
   - A response devhost cannot read becomes one sentence naming what is not running and the command that starts it, with the original text behind **Copy Error**; markup never shows (`FAIL-2`).
5. **Restart the changed services (Restart Changed, its badge icon or Alt+Ctrl+R; `POST /__devhost__/restart-service`, one write).** The footer's button names its set: **Restart 2 Changed**, with "api, web" in its tooltip and accessible description; with nothing changed, it names its fallback, the page's own service, else the stack's primary service: **Restart docs** on the docs page (`SEL-1`, `SEL-2`, `SEL-3`, `BAR-2`, `WORD-2`, `O4`, `CC-6`). The badge's icon and Alt+Ctrl+R restart the same set, read from one selector (`SEL-1`). Only services devhost can restart are ever marked changed, because an external service is never marked (`ACT-8`). With the panel open, progress and the outcome show on the rows; with it closed, the notice stack shows the activity line "Restart Changed · api, web · 1 of 2 restarted · 3 s", replaced by the outcome notice "Restarted api and web · 4.1 s", which closes 4 s after the pointer and focus leave it and stays readable in the stack's log (`FB-1`, `FB-2`, `FB-6`, `FB-7`, `FB-8`, `O14`). The changed mark clears when the new process launches with the files' content; a file saved while the restart runs marks the service again only if it differs from what the new process launched with (`ACT-8`, `CC-25`).
   - While the stack starts, shuts down, restarts or a repository switches, the button and its badge icon are unavailable with that operation and its progress as the reason, "Restart Stack is running · 3 of 6 started", and Alt+Ctrl+R shows that reason in a notice instead of doing nothing (`SEL-2`, `MENU-3`, `HOLD-3`); while another service restarts or a hot reload runs, the set queues behind it (`SEL-7`, `HOLD-3`, `CC-8`).
   - Keys: Alt+Ctrl+R (`restartServices`, configurable), hinted ⌃⌥R on macOS and Ctrl+Alt+R elsewhere from the binding's one definition, with `aria-keyshortcuts` (`KEY-2`, `KEY-13`, `BAR-4`); suspended in text entry and while a devhost menu or modal is open, and active while the page's own modal dialog is open (`KEY-1`).
6. **Start the services the run left out (`POST /__devhost__/start-service`, one write).** A not-started service sits dimmed in its group as "not started · left out of this run" (or "not started · stopped by you" after `devhost service stop`), counted in the stack's totals and listed in its group instead of a separate view (`CC-7`), with its hostname and **Start**, beside which one line of visible text names what else it starts, "also starts worker"; **Start**'s accessible name is "Start admin" and its accessible description "Also starts worker" (`SEL-1`, `SEL-7`, `WORD-2`, `KEY-10`, `ACT-2`). With two or more not-started services, the footer offers **Start 2 Services**, its members in its tooltip and accessible description; while a filter hides some of them it reads **Start 1 Service** with "1 more hidden by the filter, not included" (`BAR-2`, `BAR-3`, `WORD-2`). Each started row stays in place through queued, starting with its health check, and running; running services keep their state and are never redrawn as down (`SEL-7`, `ACT-3`, `ACT-5`). The outcome reads "Started admin and worker · 5.2 s" (`FB-3`), and the terminal prints only the URLs of what started (`FB-10`).
   - Starts and restarts of different services queue instead of disabling one another; a queued row offers **Cancel Restart** or **Cancel Start**, whose accessible name adds the target, "Cancel Restart of worker", and which takes the row out of the queue with the outcome "Cancelled the queued restart of worker" (`SEL-7`, `WORD-2`, `KEY-10`, `CC-8`).
7. **Restart the whole stack (`POST /__devhost__/restart-stack`, one write after a second step).** **Restart Stack** sits at the other end of the footer from Restart Changed, separated from it and drawn in a different button style. As a stack-wide write it keeps its operation's name as its label, lists its 6 services by name in its tooltip and accessible description, "web, api, docs, worker, admin and mail", and states their count in its first press's statement (`BAR-2`, `WORD-2`, `WORD-5`, `REV-2`). Its first press expands it in place into the statement of its reach, "Restart all 6 services with new automatic ports? Took 14 s last time.", with the checkout it runs from ("main ~/acme", `SEL-4`); the question is a group labelled by that sentence and described by the six names, "web, api, docs, worker, admin and mail" (`REV-4`, `SEL-1`). **Cancel** takes the pressed button's place, focused and taking Enter, and the confirming **Restart Stack** sits beside it (`REV-2`, `REV-4`, `KEY-9`). A double press lands on **Cancel**. Escape, or a click elsewhere in the panel that lands on no control (its heading, a row's text), cancels the question and returns focus to **Restart Stack**, the panel staying open (`REV-4`, `KEY-8`); a click on another control in the panel cancels the question, and that control takes focus and acts, so a click on **Restart** in api's row restarts api (`REV-4`). Tab past the panel's last control or Shift+Tab before its first cancels the question, closes the panel and returns focus to the Services trigger (`KEY-7`); a click outside the panel cancels the question and closes the panel, focus going where the panel's close sends it: to the trigger, or, when the panel was opened with the pointer, to the page element that had focus before (`SURF-2`, `KEY-5`, `KEY-6`). Once confirmed, the confirm button stays in place as the busy control with focus, "Restarting Stack · 2 of 6 started", and each row shows its step (`REV-4`, `KEY-11`, `FB-1`); the services runtime runs every check it can before it stops anything (`REV-11`), and the page's status stream stays open throughout (`HOLD-2`). The outcome replaces the question and takes focus on its sentence: "Restarted Acme Shop · 6 of 6 running · new automatic ports for api and web · 14 s" (`FB-3`, `FB-9`, `WORD-4`).
   - While a manifest edit is rejected, the reach line adds "uses the accepted manifest; devhost.toml line 12 is not applied" (`HOLD-6`).
   - While any other operation runs, **Restart Stack** is unavailable with that operation and its progress as its reason, "Restart api is running · health check 2 s of 30 s", and the control API refuses it with `409` naming that operation (`HOLD-3`, `HOLD-1`, `CC-37`).
   - The crash view's and recovery pages' **Restart Stack** run on one press with the same reach stated in a line beside the button (`REV-1`, `REV-2`, `BAR-2`, `CC-17`); startup-and-recovery owns those surfaces and draws their service states with this flow's shared state indicator (step 1).
   - Restart Stack has no terminal counterpart yet: `devhost stack restart` replaces the devhost process, a different operation that the reference renames `devhost stack replace` (`O18`, `WORD-1`), and the `devhost service` commands take services or groups (`CC-11`). This is a proposed exception to `MENU-6`, which asks every lifecycle action a browser surface offers to have a terminal command, until the reference names one; stack-run-stop owns the terminal commands.

Branches and edge states:

- **The badge icon or Alt+Ctrl+R with the panel closed.** Progress is one activity line in the notice stack and the outcome one notice in its place (`FB-1`, `FB-2`); opening the panel during the restart shows the same progress on the rows, and the activity line then goes, so progress shows in one place at a time.
- **A hot reload or a worktree switch restarts services.** The rows it touches show its steps, "restarting · Apply devhost.toml" or "switching to fix-nav ~/acme/.worktrees/fix-nav", never running from the old checkout and never failed; every other row keeps its state; the panel asks nothing, because a reload is reported, never reviewed, and the reload's or the switch's own flow words its outcome (`ACT-3`, `REV-10`, `SEL-5`, `FAIL-5`).
- **A restart from another tab, the terminal, an agent or a file change on disk.** Every tab draws the touched rows' transitions (`ACT-3`); a tab that did not start the operation gets an outcome notice only when it failed or touched the page's own service, with its origin ("Restarted api · 2.6 s · from the terminal") (`FB-2`, `WORD-4`, `ACT-1`); every outcome stays in the stack's log (`FB-7`).
- **The lost connection.** From the moment the status stream closes, every row keeps its last known state drawn as unknown with "last seen 16:24", the trigger shows the connection's state instead of a count, and every write in the panel, the badge icon and Alt+Ctrl+R are unavailable with the one reason the backend sent, said once in the panel's status line and given to each of them as its accessible description: after a stop, the stopped stack's sentence (next branch), otherwise "Lost connection to devhost; retrying" (`ACT-5`, `HOLD-1`, `HOLD-2`, `MENU-3`). Queued operations stay queued in the services runtime. Notices-and-feedback owns that notice, **Retry Now** and "Connected again · 6 of 6 running"; this flow draws the rows and the trigger. Neither Restart Stack nor **Start** causes a lost connection (`HOLD-2`).
- **The stack stopped.** The panel's status line shows the stack stopped in one sentence with its time, its reason and the command that starts it again, "Acme Shop stopped at 16:24 by devhost stop. Start it with devhost start in ~/acme", the same sentence every write in the panel, the badge icon and Alt+Ctrl+R take as their accessible description, never six failed services (`HOLD-1`, `HOLD-2`, `ACT-10`); devhost never starts a stack from the page.
- **A stack still starting.** Rows read "starting · health check 4 s of 60 s", or name the dependency they wait for, and the trigger reads "0 of 2 running · 2 starting" (`ACT-4`, `STATE-1`).
- **Another operation in progress.** A start or restart of another service queues behind a per-service operation or a hot reload, shown as "queued behind Restart mail" or "queued behind Apply devhost.toml" with **Cancel Restart** or **Cancel Start** on every tab, and survives page reloads and closed tabs; while the stack starts, shuts down or runs a stack-wide operation, every **Restart**, **Start** and Restart Changed is unavailable with that operation and its progress as the reason ("Starting Acme Shop · 2 of 6 started", "Stopping Acme Shop · 3 of 6 stopped", "Restart Stack is running · 3 of 6 started"); **Restart Stack** is unavailable while any other operation runs, with "Restart api is running · health check 2 s of 30 s"; a restart of a service already restarting is refused with "api is already restarting" (`SEL-7`, `HOLD-3`, `REV-12`, `CC-8`).
- **A daemon whose stop command is slow or never returns.** While a daemon's status or stop command runs, its row reads "stopping · stop command 12 s" and rows queued behind it say so. When the command passes its deadline, devhost ends it, the row reads "mail's stop command did not finish within 20 s; the daemon may still be running", the operation ends failed for mail, and the queue, manifest saves and Ctrl-C proceed (`FAIL-3`, `HOLD-3`); the deadline's value is a pending product decision (Backend 4).
- **A blocked repository.** Its group header says once why, "The checkout ~/acme/.worktrees/fix-nav was deleted; its services still run from it", and offers the ways out `MENU-5` names, led by returning to the configured checkout: a link that opens the Worktrees panel at **Return to Configured Checkout**, a link that opens its checkout list to choose another checkout (worktree-switch owns both), and **Check Again** (accessible name "Check Again fix-nav"), which reads the checkout list again, as **Refresh Worktrees** does (`GET /__devhost__/worktrees`, read only), and clears the block when the checkout loads again, stopping nothing (`HOLD-4`, `HOLD-1`, `MENU-5`, `SEL-4`). Each service of it that does not run reads "blocked", and every **Restart** of the repository is unavailable with that sentence, never drawn red (`HOLD-4`, `ACT-2`).
- **A rejected manifest edit.** Every action stays available and names the change not applied in its reach line (`HOLD-6`).
- **Chat or a save from another machine.** Chat that cannot take a message (`HOLD-5`) and saves taken only from this machine (`HOLD-7`) block nothing in this flow, which sends no chat message and saves no file.
- **A partial Restart Stack.** On a stack of 6 where api exits with code 1 before its health check, the outcome reads "Restart Stack: 5 of 6 running · api exited with code 1 before its health check" with api's lead actions beside it; the rollback puts back the services as they ran before Restart Stack, service by service and with their previous automatic ports, so the 5 healthy services keep running and api keeps its recovery page; while it restores, the busy control and the touched rows read "Restoring the services as they ran before Restart Stack", and its outcome is its own, naming what it put back: "Restored the services as they ran before Restart Stack: 5 of 6 running · api still fails" (`FAIL-1`, `UNDO-1`, `ACT-3`). Restart Stack runs the accepted manifest before and after, so its rollback never claims to restore the manifest. The `error-pages-01` stopgap ships the rollback part first.
- **A lost response.** The page reads the operation's outcome from its record on the status stream, by the `operationId` it sent, and never posts again; the server answers a repeated ID with the existing record and runs nothing again (`REV-12`, `ACT-1`). The page keeps each ID it sent in `sessionStorage`, so a page reload in the middle of an operation shows the same progress and outcome on the reloaded page (`ACT-1`, `FB-2`). A write based on state the server no longer holds, such as a restart of a service a hot reload removed, is refused with the current state, and the panel redraws from it (`REV-13`).
- **The page's own service restarts.** No write in this flow reloads the page itself, so none asks about a held reload or page state (`REV-3`). A reload the app's dev server asks for when its service comes back is held while **Hold Reloads** is on: the page never reloads and reload-control's **Reload blocked** stays held, its count kept. With it off, the chat draft, page context and configuration drafts survive the reload (`UNDO-2`), and the outcome on the reloaded page, read from the operation record by its ID, names what could not be restored, such as a mark whose element is gone (`FB-4`, `FB-2`).
- **The page's own service fails a restart started here.** The crash view opens as startup-and-recovery specifies (`SURF-3`) and carries that restart's failure; the panel, covered by the modal, closes, and the row keeps the failed state and cause for when the person returns.
- **A service killed from outside.** The row reads "terminated by SIGTERM (143) at 21:53:34, not sent by devhost" with its last lines, and after a restart its details keep "Last exit: terminated by SIGTERM (143) at 21:53:34, not sent by devhost" until the stack stops (`FAIL-3`, `FAIL-5`, `WORD-7`, `WORD-8`). A service devhost stops for an operation reads as that operation's step, never as an exit (`FAIL-5`).
- **The page's own modal dialog.** The bar shows its paused state, and a Services panel that was open stays anchored and paused with it: inert, its writes unavailable, its rows kept as they were, and its reason the one line in the notice stack, which moves into the dialog, "Paused while this page's dialog is open"; it resumes, live again with its rows updated from the published state, when the dialog closes (`SURF-11`, `KEY-7`). Alt+Ctrl+R still acts, and its activity line and outcome show in that moved notice stack (`SURF-11`, `KEY-1`).

## Backend and contract changes

**Backend** (the services runtime in `apps/devhost/internal/services` and the control server in `apps/devhost/internal/devtools`, preserving the recovery contract, one operation at a time per stack, a single-service restart keeping its automatic port, external services as status only, the accepted manifest, and every page's status stream):

1. **The published service state.** `/__devhost__/ws/health` replaces each service's boolean `status` with `state` (`not-started`, `starting`, `running`, `restarting`, `stopping`, `failed`, `exited`, `blocked`; `reachable` or `not-reachable` for an external service), `cause`, `since`, `failedDependency`, `changed` with `changedFiles`, `healthCheck` (`target`, `elapsedMs`, `timeoutMs`), `routes` (`host`, `path`), `port`, `link` (an address that answers, or none), `group`, `manifestFile` and what devhost ran (`command`, `folder`, `manifestLine`), and an external service's check (`method` and `target`), its last result and its time; the services the run left out join the same list instead of `stoppedServices`, and each blocked repository carries its reason as a sentence. Serves `journey-new-project-02`, `services-panel-02`, `services-panel-2-05`, `stack-scale-05`. Required. Contract: `CC-25`, `CC-7`.
2. **The operation record.** `restart-service`, `restart-stack`, `start-service` and `stop-service` take `operationId`, a lowercase UUID the caller generates, and answer `202 Accepted` with the record: `id`, `kind`, `targets`, `origin` (the page ID each page sends, the CLI, an agent named by `DEVHOST_CHAT_SESSION`, or the watcher), each target's `step` and `outcome`, `startedAt` and `endedAt`, streamed on `/__devhost__/ws/health` at least once a second while it runs and kept until the next operation of the same kind on the same target, so the last Restart Stack's duration feeds the question. A request repeated with an ID the server holds returns that record and runs nothing. Serves `services-panel-2-02`, `services-panel-2-03`, `keyboard-and-screen-reader-01`, `notices-and-feedback-01`. Required. Contract: `CC-37`.
3. **The queue and its cancel.** The services runtime queues a start or restart of another service behind the running per-service operation or hot reload and publishes each queued operation in order; while the stack starts, shuts down or runs a stack-wide operation, a start or restart is refused with `409` naming that operation and its progress; `POST /__devhost__/operations/<id>/cancel` cancels a queued one and answers `409` once it runs. A restart of a service that is already restarting, and a stack-wide write while another operation runs, answer `409` with `{error, service}` naming the running operation. Serves `manifest-hot-reload-2-05`, `services-panel-2-05`, `stack-scale-06`. Required. Contract: `CC-8`, `CC-37`.
4. **[discuss]: A deadline on daemon lifecycle commands.** The `status` and `stop` commands of `[services.<name>.lifecycle]` run under a deadline; while one runs, its service publishes `stopping` or `starting` with the elapsed time; on expiry devhost kills the command's process group, publishes and prints "mail's stop command did not finish within 20 s; the daemon may still be running", ends the operation failed for that service and releases the stack; shutdown cancels a running command. The deadline is a threshold the reference has no product decision for: the recommended default is 20 s, the per-service stop allowance `O30` already counts, and a per-service override in the manifest is optional later; the product owner must settle the value in Phase 7 with `O30`'s calibration, and the stack-run-stop redesign must confirm it fits its stop budget. Serves `manifest-hot-reload-2-05`. Required.
5. **Changed from content.** The watcher records a hash of each watched file at every launch of the service, recomputes it after the 200 ms debounce, and publishes `changed` with `changedFiles` only while the content differs from the launch; a restart takes the new launch as the baseline. No watcher starts for an external service, and manifest validation prints one warning for `watch` on one, naming the file, line and key, on start and on each hot reload that keeps it. Serves `journey-agent-fix-03`, `stack-scale-08`, `services-panel-2-01`, `services-panel-2-06`. Required. Contract: `CC-25`.
6. **Streams stay open.** Restart Stack's route swap and a **Start** keep every page's status stream open (for example a stream close delay on devhost's own control routes, or not reloading them), and the stream sends its reason before it closes for a stop, a proxy stop or a port move, which notices-and-feedback words. Serves `services-panel-2-04`, `stack-scale-06`, `notices-and-feedback-02`. Required. Contract: `CC-26`.
7. **Routes, codes and one error body.** `start-service` is documented, `stop-service` is added for `devhost service stop` (the browser offers no per-service **Stop**, `O10`), and every write route answers errors with one JSON body, `{error, service}`: `422` for an unknown service, an external one, or one the change cannot apply to (starting a service that runs), and `409` for a clash with another operation; a caller's mistake never answers `500` or plain text. Serves `agent-cli-journey-08`, and gives `agent-cli-journey-07`'s patch its codes. Required. Contract: `CC-37`.
8. **A complete route list.** A Go test lists the paths the control server mounts and fails for any path that `reference/control-api.md` neither documents nor lists as internal. Serves `agent-cli-journey-08`. Required.
9. **Exit records.** Each exit is published with its code, or its signal by name with the status a shell reports for it (`SIGTERM`, `143`), its time and its sender (devhost, a person, outside), with the service's own last output lines kept per service and frozen at the exit, and stays in the service's record after a restart until the stack stops; services devhost stops for an operation are published as that operation's steps. Serves `interrupted-operations-04`. Required. Contract: `CC-24`.
10. **The page's own service.** The document injection names the page's service for path routes and `serve` services, so Restart Changed's fallback and its label name it. Serves `services-panel-2-02`. Required. Contract: `CC-6`.
11. **The `devhost service` commands.** `devhost service start|stop|restart <service|group>…` call routes 2, 3 and 7 and print each outcome in both outputs; the stack-run-stop redesign builds them (`agent-cli-journey-03`). Recommended. Contract: `CC-11`.
12. **[discuss]: Check Again.** A request that asks the services runtime to run one external service's check now and publish its result and time on the health stream, answering once the check has run (read only: it changes no process and takes no `operationId`). The health stream already re-runs every external check about once a second while a page listens (`apps/devhost/internal/services/stack.go:1584-1585`, `apps/devhost/internal/devtools/server.go:392-404`), so the request can be served by an immediate check and publish; its route is not in the reference: `ACT-9`, `HOLD-1` and `MENU-5` offer **Check Again** but no `CC-n` names it, so the reference's author must add it to `CC-37`'s table with startup-and-recovery (its status page offers the same action) and worktree-switch. A blocked repository's **Check Again** needs no new route: it is the checkout-list read **Refresh Worktrees** makes (`GET /__devhost__/worktrees`), which worktree-switch makes stop nothing (`HOLD-4`). Serves the unreachable external row and the blocked repository's header (`MENU-5`). Required.

## Recommended priority

**P1, with the `error-pages-01` stopgap shipped first as a P0 fix.** The realistic trigger is ordinary: a Docker-backed or database daemon whose stop takes 45 s silently holds every restart, every manifest save and Ctrl-C, and a stop that never returns wedges the stack until devhost is killed, which leaves services and routes behind (`manifest-hot-reload-2-05`); every successful Restart Stack and every **Start** end with the whole stack drawn red, and every `devhost stop` with a tab open leaves six "crashed" services with live **Restart** buttons (`services-panel-2-04`). Restarts lose no files, so the cost is time and trust rather than data: a misread outage invites another 14 s Restart Stack, and a forgotten changed mark or an unannounced failure sends people to the terminal. The panel and Alt+Ctrl+R are used daily, more often than the terminal's lifecycle commands, and five redesigns read the state model it defines. Ship first the slice with no backend dependency: focus kept on busy controls, failures kept until handled, the in-place Restart Stack question set apart from Restart Changed, a lost connection drawn as unknown with every write unavailable, state words from today's health fields, and the panel's filter and groups. Then ship the state model and the operation record together (published states with causes, `operationId` and `202`, the queue and its cancel, streams kept open, the content-based changed mark), with the per-row progress, outcomes and **Start 2 Services** that read them; the Restart Changed fallback moves to the page's service in the same release as the label that names it, so no release restarts a service its label does not name. Last, the exit records, the documented routes with their completeness test, and the lifecycle command deadline once its value is decided. This flow ships the foundation the others cite: the published service state, the operation record (`CC-37`, `ACT-1`), the shared service state indicator and the shared state-words module (`SURF-14`, `WORD-6`). Seams with the other two foundations, with no blocker in either direction: notices-and-feedback provides the notice stack, the announcer and the lost-connection notice, and this flow draws the rows, dots and counts of that state; toolbar-shell provides the bar, the collapsed badge, the keyboard model and `Button`'s unavailable state, which read this flow's state model. Seams with three redesigns that do not depend on this one, also with no blocker in either direction: log-minimap's pinned log panel opens filtered from a row's **Logs**; config-editor's window opens from **Edit** with the row's context; reload-control's detection of an in-place update adds its note beside the changed mark. The five redesigns that build on this one depend on it: startup-and-recovery's crash view and recovery pages call this flow's restart and draw each state with its shared state indicator and words in the status pages' stylesheet; stack-directory's cards draw crashed, not started and stopped with the same indicator and words; stack-run-stop's `devhost service` commands and `devhost status` read the same record and state, and its leftover detection names the process a restart refuses for; worktree-switch reads the published service state with `blocked` and switching rows, the operation record and the shared state indicator; manifest-hot-reload's reload uses the operation record, the published states, the per-service frozen output lines and the queue, and this flow draws the rows that reload touches.
