# Watch and restart services from the toolbar: proposed experience

Diagrams: [current](../services-panel-current.html), [proposed](../services-panel-proposed.html). [Wireframe](../wireframes/services-panel.html).

Status: design proposal, not implemented behavior. The [shared UX pattern reference](../patterns.md) (revision 6) wins where this proposal disagrees with it.

Source revision: alexgorbatchev/devhost `190a8bb53e8cd903d2db0a92cb0886f29671607f` (Rust `main`). Features: TB-03, SVC-01, SVC-02, SVC-03, SVC-04, SVC-05. Redesign ticket: tracked privately.

Product rule: `stacks/recovery.md` states the recovery contract: "a service that crashes, times out, or returns errors does not take the stack down with it. Its hostname shows what went wrong, with recent logs and a restart button, and the other services keep running." The owner's rules add that external services (`managed = false`) show status only (and never block worktree switches, `O7`) and that every manifest setting applies through hot reload. So one failed service leaving the others running is intended, an external service offering no **Restart** or **Start** is intended, and a rejected manifest edit leaving the stack on its accepted manifest is intended; none of them is a defect. The gap is around them: the toolbar cannot tell starting, failed, killed, not started and disconnected apart, no restart says what it restarted or how it ended, the one stack-wide restart sits beside the everyday one without a second step, Restart Stack's rollback breaks the recovery contract when one service cannot start (`error-pages-01`, fixed first by its stopgap), manifest-free command-mode `devhost serve -- <cmd>` sessions split into fake `external` `port-<port>` services with a failing `Restart Stack` button (`serve-command-02`), and narrow viewports clip `Restart Changed`, overlap the log minimap, repeat the `Services` heading, and render button borders below `3:1` contrast (`1.63:1` dark / `1.57:1` light against card, `1.46:1` / `1.41:1` against fill; `serve-command-09`).

## Target experience

The person glances at the bar: the Services trigger counts every service of the stack and leads with what needs them ("3 of 6 running · 1 failed · 2 not started"), as one dot per service while the dots fit on the bar's row and as a fixed-width summary when they would not. Opening it keeps the page, its scroll position and the person's place: an anchored popover, clear of both the notice stack and the right-edge log minimap at every width, lists every service in its group without repeating `Services` as a subsection heading, each row with its state word, cause and time, its route as text, and its actions drawn with `>= 3:1` `--dh-input-line` button borders in both themes; focus lands in the filter when the rows overflow, else on the first row action. Pressing a row's **Restart** keeps focus on that button, which reads "Restarting api…" while the row steps through stopping, starting and "health check 2 s of 30 s"; the row then becomes the receipt, "Restarted api · 2.6 s", announced once. **Restart 2 Changed**, or **Restart docs** when nothing changed, names its set (wrapping cleanly on `320 px` viewports with its desktop shortcut pill hidden so it never clips), and Alt+Ctrl+R and a chat turn summary's **Restart Changed** (`O58`) restart the same set, reporting through an activity line and an outcome notice when the panel is closed (pausing its countdown on arrival while the browser window is unfocused, `O53`). Not-started services sit in their groups with **Start**, and **Start 2 Services** starts every one of them; starts and restarts of other services queue on their rows instead of locking each other. **Restart Stack**, set apart at the far end of the footer, expands in place into "Restart all 6 services with new automatic ports? Took 14 s last time." with **Cancel** focused; once confirmed, its button stays as the busy control, every row shows its step, and the panel ends with one outcome that counts what runs. In a manifest-free command-mode `devhost serve -- <cmd>` session, the trigger reads `1/1`, the panel attaches discovered HTTP URLs and local ports directly to the command without synthetic `external` `port-<port>` rows, omits **Restart Changed**, and offers a working **Restart Command** action instead of a failing **Restart Stack** (`serve-command-02`). A failure stays on its row with the exit, the signal, the last output lines, the probe and what devhost ran, led by the action that can fix it, until the person dismisses it or the same restart succeeds. From another environment, browsers authenticated with the shared-domain passkey or a `devhost pair` link use every control as this machine does, while unauthenticated remote browsers see writes unavailable with `"Sign in with passkey or pair this browser to use devhost here"` (`O36`, `O37`). A lost connection draws every row as unknown with the time it was last seen, never down, and makes every write unavailable with the reason. Restarts cannot be taken back, so there is no Undo: every outcome says what changed, and a failed Restart Stack restores what ran before, service by service. Benchmarks: Docker Desktop shows a partly started Compose project as "Running (3/6)" with **Start** on each stopped container and words such as "Exited (1)" for each state; Tilt keeps each resource's own state, the elapsed time and a pending update after a file change, and its trigger names the resource it updates; JetBrains' Services window filters and groups run configurations and asks "Stop and Rerun" before replacing a run; VS Code keeps a busy control focused, caps visible notifications at three and shows "Reconnecting…" with the last known state instead of an outage; Kubernetes keeps a pod Running but not Ready until its probe passes, marks pods Unknown rather than Failed when it loses their node, and reports a rollback as an outcome of its own; `docker compose up --wait` certifies readiness, not a step. Competitor behavior is evidence here, never a requirement.

## Target steps

Today: 5 steps, the last a guess: trigger, panel, scan bare names for the row, **Restart** (focus falls to the page), watch a dot turn green with no outcome. Proposed: 4 steps: trigger, panel with focus where the work is, **Restart** on the row, outcome on that row. The shortcut path stays 2 steps, but ends in a stated outcome instead of one 8 px dot spinning: press, read the activity line and its outcome.

1. **Read the stack at a glance (the published service state, read only).** The Services trigger shows one dot per service of the stack, the services the run left out included as hollow dots, while the dots fit on the bar's single row at the current window width; when they would wrap the bar, it shows a fixed-width summary instead, "3/6 · 1 failed: api · 2 not started", naming up to three services that need the person, then "+N" (`BAR-1`, `SURF-10`, `ACT-6`, `ACT-13`, `WORD-5`). On a manifest-free command-mode `devhost serve -- <cmd>` session, the trigger counts only the command itself (`1/1`), never inflating counts with synthetic `port-<port>` services (`serve-command-02`). Each dot's shape and color tell its state, the same in forced colors (`ACT-13`). Its accessible name is the summary spelled out: "Services: 3 of 6 running, 1 failed: api, 2 not started, 1 changed". The changed badge, "1 changed", and its restart icon sit beside the summary and stay there beside a failure (`SEL-2`, `ACT-8`). A service that is starting counts as starting, "0 of 2 running · 2 starting", never as failed (`ACT-4`). Every dot, row and state word this flow draws comes from one shared service state indicator (shape, state word and accessible name) and one shared module of state words, labels and sentence builders under `packages/devhost-ui/src/`, both shipped in the devtools Shadow DOM stylesheet and in the status pages' document stylesheet, so startup-and-recovery's crash view and recovery pages and stack-directory's cards draw each state with the same shape and words (`SURF-14`, `ACT-13`, `WORD-6`). When the toolbar is collapsed, toolbar-shell's badge reads the same model: it takes the look of the highest condition in `ACT-7`'s order and names what needs the person, "1 failed · 1 changed", with api's failure notice in one line beside it (`SURF-8`, `ACT-7`); a routine restart, of one row or of the whole stack, never raises it, because a transition is never a failure (`ACT-7`, `ACT-3`).
   - Defaults: dots while they fit, decided by the fit test at every viewport, never by a service count (`SURF-10`).
   - Keys: Alt+Ctrl+B focuses the bar; the trigger is an arrow stop (ArrowLeft, ArrowRight, Home, End); Enter, Space or the arrow that points toward the panel opens it (`KEY-3`, `KEY-4`).

When the rows overflow the panel's height, the panel gains a filter field, focused on open, that matches name, hostname and group as the person types and keeps group headers with their counts (`SEL-10`, `SURF-10`), and the rows that need the person (failed, exited, blocked, changed) are pinned in a group above the rest; the footer actions stay outside the scrolling area at every length (`SURF-10`, `SURF-9`). The panel is placed and sized so it never overlaps the notice stack, in either corner placement and with chat docked compact or expanded, paints above docked chat, so a failure notice beside it stays readable and clickable while it is open (`SURF-1`, `SURF-9`), and reserves `--devhost-minimap-collapsed-width` on the right whenever the log minimap is visible so the popover never covers the `12 px` right-edge minimap strip at `320 px`, `390 px`, or desktop widths (`packages/devhost-ui/src/devtools/shared/components/ToolbarPopover.tsx:84-87`, `serve-command-09`). Branches and edge states:

- **A manifest-free command-mode serve session (`serve-command-02`).** A `devhost serve -- <cmd>` session has no manifest and models the launched command first-class (`sessionKind: "command"`): the Services trigger counts `1/1`, the panel renders one row for the command (with its command string, state, cause, and all discovered HTTP URLs and local ports attached directly under it — no synthetic `external` `port-<port>` rows and no redundant `Services` subsection heading, `MENU-2`, `serve-command-09`), omits **Restart Changed** (no `watch` rules, `MENU-3`), and offers a working **Restart Command** action instead of **Restart Stack**. Activating **Restart Command** posts a manifest-free restart with an `operationId` (`202 Accepted`), steps the command row through stopping, starting, and running, refreshes its discovered HTTP routes cleanly, and reports its outcome on the row instead of failing with `"stack restart requires an accepted manifest"`. `devhost stack info --session` reads the same first-class snapshot (`O62`; `serve-folder` owns the command-mode session lifecycle and CLI framing).
- **The badge icon or Alt+Ctrl+R with the panel closed.** Progress is one activity line in the notice stack and the outcome one notice in its place (`FB-1`, `FB-2`); opening the panel during the restart shows the same progress on the rows, and the activity line then goes, so progress shows in one place at a time.
- **A hot reload or a worktree switch restarts services.** The rows it touches show its steps, "restarting · Apply devhost.toml" or "switching to fix-nav ~/acme/.worktrees/fix-nav", never running from the old worktree and never failed; every other row keeps its state; external services (`managed = false`) never block the switch and need no warning before or after it, staying untouched and marked `external` while managed services restart from the target worktree (`SEL-5`, `ACT-9`, `O7`); the panel asks nothing, because a reload is reported, never reviewed, and the reload's or the switch's own flow words its outcome (`ACT-3`, `REV-10`, `SEL-5`, `FAIL-5`, `WORD-11`).
- **A restart from another tab, the terminal, an agent or a file change on disk.** Every tab draws the touched rows' transitions (`ACT-3`); a tab that did not start the operation gets an outcome notice only when it failed or touched the page's own service, with its origin ("Restarted api · 2.6 s · from the terminal") (`FB-2`, `WORD-4`, `ACT-1`); every outcome stays in the stack's log (`FB-7`).
- **The lost connection.** From the moment the status stream closes, every row keeps its last known state drawn as unknown with "last seen 16:24", the trigger shows the connection's state instead of a count, and every write in the panel, the badge icon and Alt+Ctrl+R are unavailable with the one reason the backend sent, said once in the panel's status line and given to each of them as its accessible description: after a stop, the stopped stack's sentence (next branch), otherwise "Lost connection to devhost; retrying" (`ACT-5`, `HOLD-1`, `HOLD-2`, `MENU-3`). Queued operations stay queued in the services runtime. Notices-and-feedback owns that notice, **Retry Now** and "Connected again · 6 of 6 running"; this flow draws the rows and the trigger. Neither Restart Stack nor **Start** causes a lost connection (`HOLD-2`).
- **The stack stopped.** The panel's status line shows the stack stopped in one sentence with its time, its reason and the command that starts it again, "Acme Shop stopped at 16:24 by devhost stop. ## Backend and contract changes

**Backend** (the services runtime in `apps/devhost/src/services/` — `stack.rs`, `stack/live.rs`, `stack/runtime.rs`, `stack/command.rs`, `health.rs`, `watch.rs`, `serve_command.rs` — and the control server in `apps/devhost/src/devtools/` — `server.rs`, `streams.rs` — preserving the recovery contract, one operation at a time per stack, a single-service restart keeping its automatic port, external services as status only, the accepted manifest, and every page's status stream):

1. **The published service state.** `/__devhost__/ws/health` (`apps/devhost/src/devtools/streams.rs`, `apps/devhost/src/services/stack/live.rs`) replaces each service's boolean `status` with `state` (`not-started`, `starting`, `running`, `restarting`, `stopping`, `failed`, `exited`, `blocked`; `reachable` or `not-reachable` for an external service), `cause`, `since`, `failedDependency`, `changed` with `changedFiles`, `healthCheck` (`target`, `elapsedMs`, `timeoutMs`), `routes` (`host`, `path`), `port`, `link` (an address that answers, or none), `group`, `manifestFile` and what devhost ran (`command`, `folder`, `manifestLine`), and an external service's check (`method` and `target`), its last result and its time; the services the run left out join the same list instead of `stoppedServices`, and each blocked repository carries its reason as a sentence. Serves `journey-new-project-02`, `services-panel-02`, `services-panel-2-05`, `stack-scale-05`. Required. Contract: `CC-25`, `CC-7`.
2. **The operation record.** `restart-service`, `restart-stack`, `start-service` and `stop-service` (`apps/devhost/src/devtools/server.rs`, `apps/devhost/src/services/stack/runtime.rs`) take `operationId`, a lowercase UUID the caller generates, and answer `202 Accepted` with the record: `id`, `kind`, `targets`, `origin` (the page ID each page sends, the CLI, an agent named by `DEVHOST_CHAT_SESSION`, or the watcher), each target's `step` and `outcome`, `startedAt` and `endedAt`, streamed on `/__devhost__/ws/health` at least once a second while it runs and kept until the next operation of the same kind on the same target (or dismissed across tabs via `POST /__devhost__/operations/<id>/dismiss`, owned by `notices-and-feedback` Backend 2), so the last Restart Stack's duration feeds the question. A request repeated with an ID the server holds returns that record and runs nothing. Serves `services-panel-2-02`, `services-panel-2-03`, `keyboard-and-screen-reader-01`, `notices-and-feedback-01`. Required. Contract: `CC-37`.
3. **The queue and its cancel.** The services runtime (`apps/devhost/src/services/stack/runtime.rs`) queues a start or restart of another service behind the running per-service operation or hot reload and publishes each queued operation in order; while the stack starts, shuts down or runs a stack-wide operation, a start or restart is refused with `409` naming that operation and its progress; `POST /__devhost__/operations/<id>/cancel` cancels a queued one and answers `409` once it runs. A restart of a service that is already restarting, and a stack-wide write while another operation runs, answer `409` with `{error, service}` naming the running operation. Serves `manifest-hot-reload-2-05`, `services-panel-2-05`, `stack-scale-06`. Required. Contract: `CC-8`, `CC-37`.
4. **Shared daemon lifecycle deadline, process-group kill, and parallel shutdown (`O41`, `O30`, `manifest-hot-reload-2-06`).** In `apps/devhost/src/services/stack.rs` (`stop_daemon_lifecycle_service`, `stop_daemon_lifecycle_services`, `run_service_command_status`), a daemon's `[services.<name>.lifecycle]` `status` read and the `stop` command it leads to share one 20 s deadline counted from the `status` read's start (20 s for a stop without a status command and for the start path's status read), configurable per service via `[services.<name>.lifecycle].stopTimeout`. While one runs, its service publishes `stopping` or `starting` with the elapsed time. At the deadline or on cancellation, devhost sends `SIGTERM` and then `SIGKILL` to the command's whole process group (`-pgid`) even if the group leader exited immediately on `SIGTERM` (fixing `manifest-hot-reload-2-06`) and logs any killed leftover PIDs/names; checks cancellation/timeout before exit status so a cut `status` read reports "did not finish" rather than "not running"; publishes and prints "mail's stop command did not finish within 20 s; the daemon may still be running" followed by a fresh status read; ends the operation failed for that service; and releases the queue, manifest saves and Ctrl-C. During stack shutdown (`O30`), daemon stops run in parallel with foreground service stops from `t = 0` inside the configurable 30 s default `stopTimeout` (`5s`..`10m`); raising `stopTimeout` does not extend `O41`'s 20 s default, while a daemon `lifecycle.stopTimeout` above 20 s widens the stack's effective wait to `max(stopTimeout, max_daemon_override + 10s)` unless CLI `--timeout` caps the run. Serves `manifest-hot-reload-2-05`, `manifest-hot-reload-2-06`. Required. Contract: `CC-35`, `O30`, `O41`.
5. **Changed from content.** `ServiceWatches` (`apps/devhost/src/services/watch.rs`) records a hash of each watched file at every launch of the service, recomputes it after the 200 ms debounce, and publishes `changed` with `changedFiles` only while the content differs from the launch; a restart takes the new launch as the baseline. No watcher starts for an external service, and manifest validation (`apps/devhost/src/manifest/validation.rs`) prints one warning for `watch` on one, naming the file, line and key, on start and on each hot reload that keeps it. Serves `journey-agent-fix-03`, `stack-scale-08`, `services-panel-2-01`, `services-panel-2-06`. Required. Contract: `CC-25`.
6. **Streams stay open.** Restart Stack's route swap and a **Start** keep every page's status stream open (`apps/devhost/src/devtools/streams.rs`, `apps/devhost/src/services/stack/runtime.rs`), and the stream sends its reason before it closes for a stop, a proxy stop or a port move, which notices-and-feedback words. Serves `services-panel-2-04`, `stack-scale-06`, `notices-and-feedback-02`. Required. Contract: `CC-26`.
7. **Routes, codes and one error body.** `start-service` is documented, `stop-service` is added for `devhost service stop` (the browser offers no per-service **Stop**, `O10`), and every write route in `apps/devhost/src/devtools/server.rs` answers errors with one JSON body, `{error, service}`: `422` for an unknown service, an external one, or one the change cannot apply to (starting a service that runs), and `409` for a clash with another operation; a caller's mistake never answers `500` or plain text. Serves `agent-cli-journey-08`, and gives `agent-cli-journey-07`'s patch its codes. Required. Contract: `CC-37`.
8. **A complete route list.** A Rust test lists the paths the control server mounts in `apps/devhost/src/devtools/server.rs` and fails for any path that `reference/control-api.md` neither documents nor lists as internal. Serves `agent-cli-journey-08`. Required.
9. **Exit records.** Each exit (`apps/devhost/src/services/stack/live.rs`, `apps/devhost/src/services/stack.rs`) is published with its code, or its signal by name with the status a shell reports for it (`SIGTERM`, `143`), its time and its sender (devhost, a person, outside), with the service's own last output lines kept per service and frozen at the exit, and stays in the service's record after a restart until the stack stops; services devhost stops for an operation are published as that operation's steps. ## Recommended priority

**P1, with the `error-pages-01` stopgap shipped first as a P0 fix.** The realistic trigger is ordinary: a Docker-backed or database daemon whose stop takes 45 s silently holds every restart, every manifest save and Ctrl-C, and a stop that never returns wedges the stack until devhost is killed, which leaves services and routes behind (`manifest-hot-reload-2-05`, plus `manifest-hot-reload-2-06` for `SIGTERM`-ignoring children when the group leader exits early); manifest-free `devhost serve -- <cmd>` sessions split into fake `external` `port-<port>` services and fail `Restart Stack` with an accepted-manifest error (`serve-command-02`); every successful Restart Stack and every **Start** end with the whole stack drawn red, and every `devhost stop` with a tab open leaves six "crashed" services with live **Restart** buttons (`services-panel-2-04`); and narrow viewports clip `Restart Changed`, cover the log minimap, repeat `Services`, and drop button borders to `1.63:1` dark / `1.57:1` light against card (`1.46:1` / `1.41:1` against fill; `serve-command-09`). Restarts lose no files, so the cost is time and trust rather than data: a misread outage invites another 14 s Restart Stack, and a forgotten changed mark or an unannounced failure sends people to the terminal. The panel and Alt+Ctrl+R are used daily, more often than the terminal's lifecycle commands, and five redesigns read the state model it defines.
