# Use your framework's devtools from the toolbar: proposed experience

Diagrams: [current](../third-party-devtools-current.html), [proposed](../third-party-devtools-proposed.html). [Wireframe](../wireframes/third-party-devtools.html).

Status: design proposal, not implemented behavior. The [shared UX pattern reference](../patterns.md) wins where this proposal disagrees with it.

Source revision: alexgorbatchev/devhost `edb5c0fc01cabae490e92ac23d4c785ec896c4ed`. Features: EXT-01, EXT-02, EXT-03, EXT-04, EXT-05. Redesign ticket: tracked privately.

Product rule: the integrations' safety boundary in `docs/internal/references/external-devtools-internals.md` ("Safety boundaries") reads: "Host-mounted aggregation is scoped to launchers, not panels. devhost may hide supported native launcher controls and proxy open/close through native controls. It must not reparent, restyle wholesale, or take ownership of native panel contents." `packages/devhost-ui/src/devtools/AGENTS.md` adds for the Redux inspector: "Keep the injected Redux launcher inside devhost's Shadow DOM; never load those native inspector styles into the host document or restyle the upstream panel." So a library's panel staying where the library docks it, with its own keys, Escape and look, is intended, and so is the Redux inspector being devhost's own window rather than a panel; none of that is a defect, and this proposal keeps it. The gap is in what devhost owns around the panels: the toolbar cannot say which library an entry opens or which of two pressed panels is on screen, the obvious click to find the Redux window destroys it, a store that cannot connect shows only in hover text, devhost hides two libraries' own close buttons, a press that finds no control after a library update does nothing and says nothing, and the only opt-out also removes the Redux inspector, which has no native launcher.

## Target experience

The person's app mounts TanStack Router and Query, a React Hook Form DevTool and a Zustand store registered for Redux DevTools. On the bar, one toggle per panel names its library, "TanStack Router", "TanStack Query", "React Hook Form" and "Redux DevTools", and its tooltip says what it opens on this page; when they would wrap the bar, they fold into one **Devtools** control that lists them. Pressing "TanStack Router" opens Router's own panel at the bottom edge, and devhost's dock moves onto the panel's top edge so the last row of the route tree stays readable; pressing "TanStack Query", whose tooltip has already said "closes TanStack Router, open on the same edge", closes Router through its own close control and opens Query, so the one pressed launcher on that edge is the panel on screen. Every panel closes from its own close or minimize control, its launcher, or the library's own keys, and the dock returns to the corner the person chose. "Redux DevTools" opens devhost's inspector window on the first registered store, named in its picker and its window title, with the Zustand changes made before it opened; when the window falls behind the app, pressing the launcher again brings it to the front, and a separate ✕ beside it closes it; a page reload leaves the window waiting, then reconnects it. A store that cannot connect shows on the launcher as "1 needs setup" and in the window as one sentence naming the store and the fix; a blocked popup, or a press that finds no control or that the library ignores, is a failure notice in words; and a library version whose controls devhost cannot find keeps its launcher, unavailable as "not recognized", while the library's own launcher shows again. A team that prefers Jotai's own launcher turns off only `jotai`, and the other launchers, Redux DevTools included, stay. Nothing in this journey writes except that manifest save, so there is no Undo: a panel opened by mistake closes from the same launcher, and an integration turned off comes back by saving its key as `true` again, which hot reload applies. Benchmarks: Chrome DevTools' docked panes and drawer never overlap one another and show one drawer tab at a time; VS Code's panel area shows one view per area, switched by tabs, and its status bar never covers it; the TanStack Devtools shell already shows one plugin at a time inside one panel; the Redux DevTools extension names every store instance in its picker, keeps a bounded history per instance (its `maxAge`, 50 by default) and can keep its panel through reloads; Vue's standalone DevTools reconnects after a page reload. Competitor behavior is evidence here, never a requirement.

## Target steps

Today: 5 steps, two of them guesses: scan look-alike text buttons, hover to learn that "Form 1" is React Hook Form, press, read a panel whose last row sits under devhost's bar, and to reach another panel on the same edge, close the visible one first, because choosing the other opens it underneath. Proposed: 3 steps: read the launcher's name, press it, use the panel, with any other panel on that edge closed for you and the dock moved off the panel. The Redux path goes from 4 steps to 2: press, read the named store; bringing back a hidden window is one press instead of a close and a reopen that loses Zustand history.

1. **Find the launcher (the page's mounted devtools, read only).** The launchers keep their place in the bar's order, which toolbar-shell keeps: one toggle per library panel the page mounts, each named by the product name of the library whose panel it opens, and the two that open a tool for several libraries by the tool's name: "TanStack Router", "TanStack Query", "TanStack Devtools" (the unified shell with its Form, Table and Pacer plugins), "React Hook Form", "Jotai", "Vue" and "Redux DevTools", which inspects the Redux Toolkit and Zustand stores the page registers (`WORD-3`; `language-06`; `third-party-devtools-06`). An instance name appears only when the page mounts two or more panels of one library, after " · ": a React Hook Form panel takes its form's name where its panel exposes one ("React Hook Form · contact", from the `DevTool`'s `id` prop, Backend 4), else its number in discovery order, as documented; Jotai keeps numbers ("Jotai 1", "Jotai 2") because devhost collects no atom data (`integrations/jotai.md`) and so has nothing else to name a store by. A lone panel has no number, and no label reads "Form". Each launcher's tooltip and accessible description say what it opens and that it acts on this page: "Open the TanStack Query devtools of this page", or "Close the TanStack Query devtools of this page" while pressed (`SEL-3`; `selection-03`), as toolbar-shell words the scope of the other bar items. A pressed launcher reads open in the bar's active look, told by shape as well as color, so it holds in forced colors (`ACT-13`).
   - Defaults: entries in the order devhost finds the libraries, as today; inline while they fit on the bar's one row, folded into **Devtools** when they would wrap it (step 6).
   - Keys: Alt+Ctrl+B focuses the bar; each launcher is an arrow stop (ArrowLeft, ArrowRight, Home, End); Enter or Space toggles it; Tab leaves the bar (`KEY-3`, `KEY-4`; `keyboard-03`, `keyboard-04`). devhost binds none of the libraries' keys: Vue DevTools' Alt+Shift+D and TanStack Devtools' Ctrl+~ and its held Shift+Alt+Mod stay the libraries' own, and `guides/devtools.md`'s Keyboard section lists them as keys devhost leaves alone (`KEY-2`, `CC-29`; `keyboard-02`).
2. **Open a library panel (the library's own open control, read only).** Pressing a launcher clicks the library's own open control, as today; nothing devhost draws opens, and the panel is the library's, where the library docks it. Panels docked to the same window edge take turns: when another integrated panel is open on the edge the chosen panel docks to, devhost first closes it through its own close control, then opens the chosen one, so at most one launcher per edge reads pressed and it is the panel on screen (`SEL-11`, `SEL-1`; `selection-14`; `third-party-devtools-01`, this redesign's slice). The launcher names that reach before the press: its tooltip and accessible description read "Open the TanStack Query devtools of this page · closes TanStack Router, open on the same edge" (`SEL-1`). A panel's edge is read from its own rectangle while it is open: it docks to an edge when it touches that edge of the window and spans more than half of it, so TanStack Router and Query, the unified shell and Jotai dock to the bottom by default and a React Hook Form panel opens a full-height column on the right; panels on different edges stay open together. devhost never moves, resizes or restyles a panel to make room (product rule).
   - The dock steps aside: while a panel docks to the bottom edge, toolbar-shell moves the dock onto the panel's top edge, or to the top of the window when the panel is taller than half of it, follows the panel's own resize handle and returns the dock to the person's placement when the panel closes, without saving the move, so the last row of a panel's list is fully visible when scrolled to its end (`SURF-1`, `SURF-7`, `SURF-5`; `surfaces-01`, `surfaces-07`; `third-party-devtools-01`, toolbar-shell's slice). This flow supplies the seam: every adapter reports its open panel's element and edge, which the dock measures with a `ResizeObserver` (Backend 3). A React Hook Form column docked on the right meets the dock and docked chat, which `SURF-1` does not place yet; that is recorded for the reference.
   - The press changes panels only: no notice, no log line and no announcement, because opening a panel is a read, not an operation (section 1 of the reference). The pressed states are the result: the launcher whose panel was closed unpresses in the same frame as the chosen one presses.
   - Focus stays on the pressed launcher; devhost moves no focus into a library's panel, which the person reaches with the pointer or the page's own Tab order, and Alt+Ctrl+B brings focus back to the bar (`KEY-6`, `KEY-3`; `keyboard-10`). After a pointer press the page's keys keep working, the libraries' hotkeys included, because a bar item keeps only Enter, Space, the arrow keys, Home, End and Escape (`KEY-5`, `CC-28`; `keyboard-06`).
3. **Use the panel and close it (the library's own controls, read only).** The panel keeps its own keys, Escape and close controls; devhost adds no Escape to a library's panel (`KEY-1`, `KEY-8`). Every panel shows its own close or minimize control: devhost's stylesheet hides only the floating launchers, so TanStack Query's minimize button ("Close tanstack query devtools") and the unified shell's "Close TanStack Devtools" button stay visible, as Router's chevron, React Hook Form's ✕ and Jotai's minimize button already do (`third-party-devtools-02`). Closing a panel by its own control, its pressed launcher, Escape or a library hotkey unpresses its launcher within one frame, as today, and the dock returns to the person's placement. With the toolbar collapsed the launchers are hidden, and an open panel still closes from its own control; an open panel is not a condition, so the collapsed badge does not show it (`SURF-8`).
   - Picks reach the panel: an open library panel is part of the page, where devhost draws nothing, so Select Elements and Alt + right-click resolve its elements like any other page element, and a pick's element path shows the library's root, so the person can point an agent at a query's state (`SEL-9`, `SURF-11`; `selection-09`).
4. **Open the Redux inspector (`/__devhost__/redux`, read only).** Pressing **Redux DevTools** opens devhost's own inspector in a popup window, as today, now on the first registered store, named in the store picker, never on "Select..."; the person's later choice is kept while the window lives (`STATE-2`; `language-15`; `third-party-devtools-04`). While the inspector's own "Autoselect instances" is chosen, a line devhost draws above the inspector, outside the upstream panel, names the store it shows: "Following the latest change: Zustand counter". The window's title names the store, the stack and the page's host: "Redux DevTools · Zustand counter · Acme Shop (shop.localhost)", so two stacks' windows differ in the window switcher (`WORD-3`; `third-party-devtools-04`). While the window connects, its line reads "Connecting to shop.localhost…" and the launcher's description says the same (`STATE-1`).
   - Zustand history starts when the store registers, not when the window connects: the page's registration keeps each Zustand store's latest changes, at most 50 (Backend 6, **[discuss]**: the bound is a new product decision), so a window opened after three clicks lists those three `zustand/setState` changes after `@@INIT`, and closing and reopening the window keeps them; once a store has dropped older changes, the window's line says so: "Zustand counter: showing its latest 50 changes" (`third-party-devtools-03`). Redux Toolkit stores keep their own full history, as today.
   - The tooltip of the unpressed launcher names the stores it will show: "Open Redux DevTools for this page's 2 stores: Redux Toolkit counter, Zustand counter" (`SEL-1`).
5. **Bring the window back, keep it through reloads, close it (read only).** While the window is open, **Redux DevTools** reads pressed, and pressing it brings the window to the front (`focus()` on the open window) and never closes it; its tooltip reads "Show the Redux DevTools window · 2 stores connected" (`SEL-11`; `selection-14`; `third-party-devtools-03`). Closing is a control of its own: **Close Redux DevTools**, a ✕ of at least 24 by 24 CSS pixels beside the pressed launcher and its own arrow stop, whose accessible name is that label; the window's own close does the same, and either unpresses the launcher within 100 ms, as today (`SEL-11`, `SURF-9`, `KEY-4`, `WORD-2`).
   - Reloading the page, devhost's own reloads after a restart or a switch included, or opening another page of the same origin in the same tab, keeps the window: on `pagehide` the page tells the window it is leaving instead of closing it, the window reads "Waiting for shop.localhost to load again · 3 s" with the elapsed time (`STATE-1`), and the next page's toolbar reattaches to it through the session it keeps in that tab's `sessionStorage`, with the chosen store and the inspector's tab kept (Backend 5). A loaded page that registers no store leaves "This page registers no stores; this window connects when one registers" in the window (`STATE-2`). When the tab closes or leaves the origin, the window reads "The page that opened this window is gone" with **Close Window**, and the stores are untouched.
   - Turning `redux` or the whole integration off, or unmounting the toolbar, still closes the window and leaves the stores untouched, as today.
6. **Fold the launchers when they would wrap the bar (the Devtools control, read only).** By the bar's fit test, second in toolbar-shell's sequence after the Services summary, the launchers fold into one **Devtools** control when they would wrap the bar's single row, never by a count (`SURF-10`, `SEL-10`; `selection-13`, `surfaces-08`). Its label counts what is open, "Devtools · 1 open", and shows the problem look with its count while a store needs setup, "Devtools · 1 needs setup"; its accessible name spells the summary out, "Devtools: 6 on this page, TanStack Query open", and its tooltip lists each open panel. Enter, Space, the arrow that points toward the panel, or a click opens an anchored panel (`SURF-2`) listing every launcher as a row in the bar's order: the toggle named by its library, its state in words ("open", "detached", "1 needs setup"), and for Redux DevTools while its window is open, **Show** and **Close**, whose accessible names add their target ("Show Redux DevTools", "Close Redux DevTools"). The panel stays open while rows toggle, so panels on different edges open in one visit. When its rows overflow its height (a page with many React Hook Form panels), it gains a filter field, focused on open, that matches library and instance names as the person types, with "No devtools match "billing"" and **Clear Filter** for an empty result (`SEL-10`, `STATE-2`). It never overlaps the notice stack and paints above docked chat (`SURF-1`). At every acceptance viewport (1600x1000, 1280x800 and 900x900 in the light and the dark theme, and 1280x800 at 200% zoom) the bar keeps one row, the **Devtools** panel fits the viewport with every row reachable, and every launcher, **Close Redux DevTools** and **Devtools** is at least 24 by 24 CSS pixels or spaced as WCAG 2.5.8 accepts (`SURF-9`; `surfaces-09`). This folded panel is the launcher surface that `SURF-2` lists among the toolbar panels; inline launchers are bar toggles (a reading recorded for the reference).
   - Keys: on open, focus goes to the filter when there is one, else the first row's toggle (`KEY-6`); Tab moves through each row's controls in row order; Tab past the last control or Shift+Tab before the first closes the panel and returns focus to **Devtools** (`KEY-7`; `keyboard-11`); Escape or a click outside closes it and returns focus to **Devtools**, or, when it was opened with the pointer, to the page element that had focus before (`KEY-5`, `KEY-6`, `KEY-8`, `KEY-10`; `keyboard-06`, `keyboard-10`).
7. **A launcher that cannot act now says why (read only).** An installed library whose panel the toolbar cannot open or close right now keeps its launcher in place, `aria-disabled`, with a short state after its name and one reason sentence as its tooltip, its accessible description and its row in the **Devtools** panel, never removed and never live-looking (`MENU-3`, `HOLD-1`; `selection-11`):
   - The unified shell detached into its own window: "TanStack Devtools · detached", with "TanStack Devtools is detached in its own window; close that window to dock it again." Today the entry disappears.
   - Vue with the Vite DevTools dock hidden: "Vue · dock hidden", with "The Vite DevTools dock is hidden; press Alt+Shift+D to show it, then choose Vue." With the dock not authorized: "Vue · not authorized", with "The Vite DevTools dock is not authorized; select Unauthorized in the dock and enter the code Vite printed." With two dock entries sharing Vue's title: "Vue · ambiguous", with "Two Vite DevTools dock entries are titled Vue; devhost opens neither until the title is unique." Today the entry disappears in all three.
   - A library version whose controls devhost cannot find: detection requires the control the launcher's next press needs, the open control while the panel is closed and the close control while it is open, so a TanStack Router panel whose toggle devhost cannot find reads "TanStack Router · not recognized", with "devhost does not recognize this TanStack Router devtools version; use the library's own button, which devhost now shows." While any of a library's launchers is not recognized, devhost stops hiding that library's own launchers, so its panel still opens from the page; they hide again once devhost recognizes the controls (`MENU-3`, `HOLD-1`, `MENU-5`; `third-party-devtools-07`). Today the entry stays live, a press does nothing, and devhost can keep hiding the library's own launcher. React Hook Form's open state reads its panel's own visibility, never its launcher's absence, so a moved launcher never makes a closed inspector read open. Jotai's detection already reads the controls it presses, so a Jotai release that changes them removes the entry and leaves Jotai's own launcher in place, as today.
   - On the inline bar the visible reason is the short state after the name, and the sentence shows in the tooltip, the accessible description and the **Devtools** row, never as a notice: these states come from the library, its version or the person's choice (Detach, a hidden dock), they last until the person acts in the library or changes its version, and a notice for each would crowd the notice stack (`FB-8`); a press that fails is an event, which step 8 reports as a notice. This is a proposed exception to `HOLD-1`'s status line for bar items, recorded for the reference.
   - The launcher becomes available again by itself when its condition ends, without a page reload, and a focused launcher that becomes unavailable keeps focus (`HOLD-1`, `KEY-11`). A library that unmounts, or a Vite dock with no Vue app, has no launcher, because there is nothing to open (`MENU-3`).
8. **Report failures in words where they started (read only).** A launcher that fails says so as a failure notice in the notice stack, which is the dock's status line, announced once in the assertive region and kept until the person dismisses it or the same launcher succeeds (`FAIL-2`, `FB-6`, `FB-9`, `WORD-7`; `feedback-07`, `language-09`):
   - A popup the browser blocks: the launcher stays unpressed, and the notice reads "Redux DevTools could not open: the browser blocked its window. Allow popups for shop.localhost, then choose Redux DevTools again." A store's setup problem never masks it (`third-party-devtools-04`).
   - A press that finds no control, or that the library ignores (`third-party-devtools-07`). Detection usually finds a missing control before any press (step 7); a press that still finds none, because the control went away in between, knows it at once, because each adapter's open and close report what they did (Backend 2): "TanStack Router did not open: devhost found no open control for it in this devtools version. Use the library's own button, which devhost now shows.", and the launcher turns "not recognized". When the press clicked a control but the panel's state still differs after 60 frames (about a second), the library ignored it: "TanStack Router did not open: its open control did not respond. Use the library's own button, which devhost now shows." A close reads "did not close" and ends "Close it from the panel itself." A Vite DevTools dock that refuses the switch reads "Vue did not open: the Vite DevTools dock refused to switch to it. Use the dock's own Vue entry, which devhost now shows.", with the dock's message behind **Copy Error** (`FAIL-2`, `WORD-7`, `MENU-5`). Each notice links its integration page of devhost's docs ("TanStack integration"), which lists the versions devhost is tested with and the key that turns that integration off (step 9). Until a press of that launcher succeeds, devhost stops hiding that library's own launchers, so the way out the notice names is on screen; the press that succeeds hides them again and clears the notice. Focus stays on the launcher, whose accessible description becomes the failure sentence (`KEY-11`), and pressing it again with the same result updates that notice with a count instead of adding another (`FAIL-4`). Today the press ends in silence: no notice, no new tooltip and no console line.
   - These are page actions, not operations of the stack, so they get no line in the stack's log (`FB-7` covers operations); the notice is their record until dismissed (a reading recorded for the reference).
   - A store that cannot connect is a state, not an event: the launcher keeps working for the other stores and shows the problem by shape and text, "Redux DevTools · 1 needs setup", with "Redux DevTools: 2 stores connected; Cart store needs setup" as its tooltip and accessible description, never "unavailable" (`ACT-13`, `WORD-7`; `third-party-devtools-04`). Opening the window shows, above the inspector, "Cart store cannot connect: it has no Redux DevTools instrumentation. Add instrument() to it once, or register a store already set up for the Redux DevTools extension." with **Copy Error**, which holds the original message (`FAIL-2`). When every store fails, the window still opens on that line.
9. **Turn one integration off (a save of a manifest file, one write, applied by hot reload).** `[devtools.externalToolbars]` keeps `enabled` as the master switch and gains one key per integration, `tanstack`, `redux`, `vue`, `reactHookForm` and `jotai`, each `true` by default (`SEL-11`; `selection-14`; `third-party-devtools-05`). Setting one to `false` removes that integration's launchers and stops hiding its native launcher, which comes back, and leaves every other launcher; open panels stay open, as today. `redux = false`, like `enabled = false`, removes Redux DevTools, which has no native launcher, and the description of each key in the example manifest, `guides/devtools.md` and each integration page says what it removes. The save applies through hot reload without a page reload and asks nothing (`REV-1`, `REV-10`); the stack's terminal and log print the reload's one outcome line, "Applied devhost.toml: turned off the Jotai launchers · nothing restarted" (`FB-3`, `FB-10`), the line toolbar-shell words for every toolbar change. An unknown key in the table is reported with its file, line and key, as today. Restored native launchers sit where their libraries put them; keeping devhost's dock off them is toolbar-shell's per-person placement (`toolbar-shell-02`).

Branches and edge states:

- **The page's own modal dialog.** While the page shows a modal dialog, the launchers pause with the bar, inert, and the reason is one line in the moved notice stack, "Paused while this page's dialog is open" (`SURF-11`, `CC-3`); library panels and an open Redux window stay as they are, and a **Devtools** panel open when the dialog opens stays anchored and paused until it closes (`KEY-7`).
- **Lost connection.** While the page's status stream is lost, every library launcher keeps working, because it acts in the page; **Redux DevTools** is unavailable while its window is closed, because devhost serves that window's page, with the lost connection's sentence ("Lost connection to devhost; retrying") as its accessible description and the condition notice as its visible reason (`HOLD-2`, `HOLD-1`); an open window keeps working, because it talks to the page directly.
- **Panels on two edges.** A React Hook Form column on the right and TanStack Query at the bottom stay open together; both launchers read pressed, and each closes on its own.
- **A panel docked to the top or a side.** An app that docks TanStack Query to the top edge with the library's own `position` setting gets the same turn-taking on that edge; the dock moves only for a panel at the bottom edge (`SURF-1`).
- **Several instances of one library.** Two React Hook Form panels without an `id` read "React Hook Form 1" and "React Hook Form 2" in discovery order, renumbered on remount and from 1 after a reload, as documented; with `id` props they read their names.
- **Two tabs or two stacks.** Each tab keeps its own Redux window session; window titles name store, stack and host, so two stacks' windows, or windows of pages on different hosts, never read alike.
- **A narrow window.** Below the acceptance viewports the **Devtools** panel takes the window's width less 16 px on each side (`O8`).
- **No integration on the page.** The page shows no launcher segment and no **Devtools** control.

## What changes versus today

- **Removed:** hiding TanStack Query's minimize button and the unified shell's close button; the pressed **Redux** entry closing the window; closing the Redux window on every page reload or same-origin navigation; a number on a lone panel ("Jotai 1") and the "Form" label; two launchers on one edge reading pressed while one panel hides the other; entries that vanish while the shell is detached or Vue's dock is hidden or unauthorized, replaced by unavailable launchers with their reason; "Redux DevTools unavailable: …" as the title while other stores connect; the setup error and the blocked popup living only in hover text; a press that finds no control or that the library ignores ending in silence, and devhost hiding the own launcher of a library it cannot drive, replaced by failure notices and the "not recognized" state; React Hook Form's open state inferred from its launcher's absence, replaced by its panel's visibility; the all-or-nothing opt-out, replaced by one key per integration under the kept master switch.
- **Merged:** the launcher segment and its overflow become one list with two forms, inline and the **Devtools** panel, read from one launcher model (state, name, reach, reason).
- **New:** library names on every launcher; per-edge turn-taking with its reach named before the press; the open panel's element reported to toolbar-shell's dock; **Close Redux DevTools**; the Redux window's store line, title, waiting and gone states, reattachment through `sessionStorage` and the setup line with **Copy Error**; a bounded Zustand history from registration; the **Devtools** control and panel with its filter; unavailable launchers with reasons, "not recognized" among them for a library version whose controls devhost cannot find; adapters that report what a press did; failure notices for a blocked popup, a press that finds no control and a press the library ignores; the `tanstack`, `redux`, `vue`, `reactHookForm` and `jotai` keys.
- **Changed:** pressing the pressed **Redux DevTools** shows the window instead of closing it (`SEL-11`); the Redux window survives reloads of its page (`integrations/redux.md` changes); Zustand history starts at registration, not at connection; opening a panel on an occupied edge closes the other one (`integrations/jotai.md` and `integrations/tanstack.md` change); labels change from "Router", "Query", "TanStack", "Form 1", "Jotai 1" and "Redux" to library and tool names, so the stories and tests that assert today's labels change with them; the monitor's title moves from the Go constant "Redux DevTools" to a title the window sets from the page it serves; detection requires the control a launcher's next press needs, so an entry whose control is missing reads "not recognized" instead of staying live; React Hook Form's inspector reads open only while its panel is visible, so the detector's browser test "reports an inspector open while its launcher is gone, also past a stylesheet Emotion inserted" changes to assert the panel's visibility.
- **Kept:** walking every safeguard of the current flow:
  - Native launchers stop covering the page: one toolbar segment proxies open and close through each library's own controls, and the panels stay the libraries' own, never reparented, moved, resized or restyled (product rule); devhost now hides only the launchers of libraries whose controls it recognizes.
  - The pressed state follows the panels: a DOM observer keeps launchers in step when a panel closes from its own control, Escape or a hotkey, now also for Query's and the shell's restored close buttons.
  - Launchers appear and disappear as libraries mount and unmount, without a page reload; standalone Query and Router adapters ignore the unified shell's plugins, so nothing appears twice.
  - Vue is offered only from a trusted, visible dock with one Vue entry, and devhost never bypasses the dock's authorization (`integrations/vue.md`); it now says which condition is unmet.
  - The Redux inspector needs no browser extension; its window accepts a connection only from its opener, at the same origin, with the matching session ID; closing the window unpresses the launcher within 100 ms; Redux Toolkit history survives a reopen.
  - devhost never weakens popup blockers, a page's Content Security Policy or its opener policy (`integrations/redux.md`); a blocked popup is now reported, not worked around.
  - The opt-out applies by hot reload without a page reload and opens or closes no library panel; turning off Redux or the toolbar closes the window and leaves the stores untouched.
  - devhost creates, instruments or changes no store and collects no atom data; registering the same `id` again replaces the earlier registration.
  - devhost binds none of the libraries' keys and adds no Escape to their panels; it does not proxy controls into detached documents.
  - The Redux launcher stays inside devhost's Shadow DOM, and the inspector's own styles stay in its window.

## Backend and contract changes

**Backend** (the manifest in `apps/devhost/internal/manifest`, the injected configuration from `apps/devhost/internal/services` and `apps/devhost/internal/devtools`, and the integrations under `packages/devhost-ui/src/devtools/features/externalDevtoolsPanel/` and `features/reduxDevtools/`, preserving the safety boundary that devhost proxies only launchers and never takes over a library's panel, hot reload for every setting, the inspector's same-origin and session checks, and stores devhost never changes):

1. **One key per integration.** `tableKeys["devtools.externalToolbars"]` accepts `enabled`, `tanstack`, `redux`, `vue`, `reactHookForm` and `jotai`; the manifest's `ExternalToolbars` becomes a struct of six booleans, each `true` when omitted, and an integration runs only while both `enabled` and its own key are `true`; the injected configuration and the health stream's configuration update carry them as `externalToolbars: {enabled, tanstack, redux, vue, reactHookForm, jotai}` in place of `externalToolbarsEnabled`; the reload's outcome line names the integrations a save turned on or off. Serves `third-party-devtools-05`. Required. Contract: no `CC-n` yet (below).
2. **A launcher model with names, reach and reasons.** `IExternalDevtoolsAdapter` gains `library` (the product name), `instanceName` (or none), `unavailableReason` (a sentence, or none) and `readPanel()` (the open panel's element, or none), and the launcher the bar renders gains `edge` (`bottom`, `top`, `left`, `right` or none, measured from the panel's rectangle), `closes` (the launchers its press will close) and `problem` (a count and a sentence, Redux only); `toggleLauncher` closes the open panels on the chosen panel's edge through their adapters before it opens the chosen one. `open()` and `close()` return what they did, `pressed` or `no control` (the Vue adapter's a promise that rejects with the dock's message), so `toggleLauncher` raises step 8's failure at once when nothing was pressed or the dock rejected, and when the state still differs after 60 frames; a repeated failure of the same launcher updates its notice's count; until a press of that launcher succeeds, its integration's hide selectors stop applying. The hide selectors keep only floating launchers: `.tsqd-minimize-btn` and the shell's `tsd-close-button` leave them. Serves `third-party-devtools-01`, `third-party-devtools-02`, `third-party-devtools-06`, `third-party-devtools-07`. Required.
3. **The dock seam.** The launchers' hook publishes the open panels with their elements and edges to toolbar-shell's dock placement, which observes the bottom panel's size with a `ResizeObserver` and never saves the temporary move (`SURF-1`, `SURF-7`). Serves `third-party-devtools-01` (toolbar-shell's slice builds the move). Required.
4. **[discuss]: React Hook Form instance names.** Whether React Hook Form 4.4.0's panel exposes its `DevTool`'s `id` or its form in the DOM is unverified (`integrations/react-hook-form.md` says devhost does not read them today); if it does not, entries keep their numbers without "Form", and naming them needs an upstream change. The integration's owners confirm it on the fixture. Serves `third-party-devtools-06`. Recommended.
5. **The Redux window's session across reloads.** On `pagehide` the page sends the window a leaving message instead of closing it; the page keeps the session ID in `sessionStorage`; the window, while disconnected, posts its hello to `opener` every 500 ms, reads `opener.closed` and whether `opener` is still same-origin, and shows its waiting, no-stores and gone states; a page accepts a hello only from a window at its origin with the session ID it keeps, as today's `receiveHello` checks; the window sets its own title from the stack name, the page's host and the chosen store, which the page sends on connect (`stackName` from the injected configuration), and `redux_assets.go` keeps "Redux DevTools" only as the title before it connects; the window's store line, setup line and **Copy Error** are devhost's own markup in the window's document, outside the upstream inspector. Serves `third-party-devtools-03`, `third-party-devtools-04`. Required. Contract: no `CC-n` yet (below).
6. **[discuss]: Zustand history from registration.** The registration of a Zustand store keeps its latest changes from registration time, at most 50, and replays them after `@@INIT` when a window connects; the bound is a threshold no product decision sets: the recommended default is 50, the Redux DevTools extension's own `maxAge` default, and the product owner must settle it in Phase 7 with a calibration of memory per change on large states. Serves `third-party-devtools-03`. Required.
7. **Monitor preselection.** The window's monitor store selects the first registered instance when its first `INIT` arrives and keeps the person's choice across reconnects, by name and `id`. Serves `third-party-devtools-04`. Required.
8. **Unavailable states from the adapters.** The unified shell's adapter reports `detached` while its root has no panel and a Document Picture-in-Picture window is open; the Vue adapter reports `dock hidden`, `not authorized` and `ambiguous` from the conditions it already reads (`createVueDevtoolsDetector.ts`), instead of removing the entry. Every adapter that clicks the page's markup also checks the control its next press needs, the open control while its panel is closed and the close control while it is open, and reports `not recognized` when it finds the panel without it: Router and Query count as installed from their panel and then check their controls, and the shell's detection no longer requires its open button to count it installed; while any of an integration's adapters reports `not recognized`, that integration's hide selectors stop applying. React Hook Form's `isOpen()` reads its panel wrapper's visibility instead of its launcher's absence. Serves the unavailable launchers of step 7 (`MENU-3`) and `third-party-devtools-07`. Required.

## Recommended priority

**P2.** The realistic trigger is ordinary use of two or more inspectors: opening TanStack Query while Router is open buries Router under Query with both launchers pressed, and the next press on Router closes or opens a panel nobody can see (`third-party-devtools-01`); the Redux window falls behind the app within seconds, and the obvious press to bring it back closes it, so the reopened window has lost its store choice and every Zustand change made before (`third-party-devtools-03`); a team that wants one library's own launcher must turn off every integration, the Redux inspector included (`third-party-devtools-05`). Nothing here touches the app's data or the stack: stores and panels are never changed, so the cost is lost inspection history, a wrong panel read as the right one, setup errors missed because they live in hover text, and, after a devtools update the adapters were not written for, a launcher that does nothing and says nothing for everyone on that project (`third-party-devtools-07`, rarer). These inspectors are opened less often than the Services panel or chat, which is why this redesign ranks below the P1 flows. Ship first the slice with no backend dependency, all in the page: library names on the launchers, turn-taking per edge with its reach named, the restored close buttons, **Redux DevTools** that shows instead of closing with **Close Redux DevTools** beside it, preselection, the window title and the setup line, failure notices for a blocked popup, a press that finds no control and a press the library ignores, and unavailable launchers with reasons, "not recognized" included. Then the per-integration keys, a manifest change that ships with its docs in the same change; then the Redux window kept across reloads; and last the Zustand history from registration, once Phase 7 settles its bound. This redesign depends on toolbar-shell, the toolbar foundation, which ships first or with the slice that needs it: its bar makes the launchers arrow stops, its notice slot holds the failure notices, its one-row fit sequence folds the launchers into **Devtools**, and its dock moves onto a bottom panel, toolbar-shell's slice of `third-party-devtools-01`; this redesign's panel report (Backend 3) ships in its first slice so the dock can use it.
