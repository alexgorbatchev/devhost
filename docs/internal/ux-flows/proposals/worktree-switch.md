# Switch the stack to another checkout: proposed experience

Diagrams: [current](../worktree-switch-current.html), [proposed](../worktree-switch-proposed.html). [Wireframe](../wireframes/worktree-switch.html). Research: [notes](worktree-switch-research.md).

Status: design proposal, not implemented behavior. The [shared UX pattern reference](../patterns.md) wins where this proposal disagrees with it.

Source revision: alexgorbatchev/devhost `edb5c0fc01cabae490e92ac23d4c785ec896c4ed`. Features: WT-01, WT-02, WT-03, WT-04, WT-05, WT-06. Redesign ticket: tracked privately.

Product rule: a worktree switch never falls back silently to another checkout. `stacks/worktrees.md` says "devhost does not fall back to another checkout by itself", and `apps/devhost/AGENTS.md` ("Service supervision boundary") says "Missing saved selections remain stopped for explicit recovery; never silently fall back or leave a partially switched group running." The same page documents the switch's reach, "it stops every managed service", and **Stop stack**, which stops another running stack so its checkout can be picked (kept by `O10`). So a switch that restarts the whole stack, a failed switch that restores the checkout it left and never a third one, a deleted checkout that devhost never replaces by itself, and a browser that can stop another stack are intended; none of them is a defect. The gap is everything around them: nothing names the switch's reach before the one-click press or in its result, the switch throws away the run's service selection against `guides/stack-lifecycle.md`, conversations and agents in the old checkout drop out of sight, **Stop stack** ends someone else's stack with no question, no attribution and a 15 s kill, a read of the checkout list stops services, and the recovery state the rule produces leads with an action that cannot work instead of the explicit choice the rule asks for.

## Target experience

The person opens **Worktrees** from the bar, which names the branch the stack runs ("main"), and keeps the page, its scroll position and their place: an anchored panel opens with focus in **Find checkout** at its top and one shared checkout list below it, the main checkout first, then each worktree by branch, every row showing its branch (or "Detached" with a 7-character commit ID), its short commit ID, its path shortened with `~` and its badges as shapes and words. Typing filters by branch, path or the start of a commit ID; the arrows move the highlight and Enter picks the row without switching anything. Under the list, a details line says why the highlighted row is unavailable, in use or blocked and offers the way forward (**Open in Editor** at the failing line, **Copy Error**, **Open Stack**, **Stop Stack**, **Check Again**). The footer's **Switch and Restart Stack** carries a reach line that names everything the press will do: "Restarts all 6 services of Acme Shop from fix-nav ~/acme/.worktrees/fix-nav with new automatic ports · took 14 s last time", keeping the services the run was started with. One press switches when nothing else is at stake; when an agent works or waits for the person in this checkout, or the page holds unsaved work, the press expands in place into one question that names each item, with **Answer First** focused and **Switch Anyway** beside it. While the switch runs, the pressed button keeps focus and counts its steps ("Switching to fix-nav · 3 of 6 stopped · 5 s"), the panel lists each step with the service it waits on, and the bar still names main with "switching to fix-nav", never claiming the target before it runs. The page then reloads at the same URL and starts with focus on one outcome: "Switched acme from main ~/acme to fix-nav ~/acme/.worktrees/fix-nav: restarted 6 services · 6 of 6 running · 14 s", naming anything the switch left behind ("Claude Code · Fix the nav still waits for you in main") and linking the previous checkout so the way back is one switch away. A failed start returns to the previous checkout service by service and says so in the panel ("Returned to main ~/acme: 6 of 6 running"), with the failing service's cause and **Retry Switch**. A deleted running checkout reads as deleted, never preselected, and leads with **Return to Configured Checkout** in the toolbar and on every recovery page, while reading the list stops nothing. A switch restarts processes and cannot be taken back, so there is no Undo: its rollback (`UNDO-1`) restores the previous checkout when the target fails, and its outcome names the previous checkout as the way back (`UNDO-5`). Benchmarks: VS Code, JetBrains IDEs and GitHub Desktop show a branch name first with a short commit ID, search branches by name, path or commit, never preselect a repository that is gone, and switch a clean checkout without asking while asking when local work would be lost; JetBrains asks "Stop and Rerun" before replacing a run; Git itself lists worktrees whose folder is gone as `prunable` and leaves removing them to `git worktree prune`; `docker compose up <service>` keeps a project's chosen services when it is brought up again; Kubernetes reports a rollback as an outcome of its own and keeps old replicas serving until new ones are ready; Vercel and Netlify give each branch its own URL instead of switching one environment, which devhost's "Several worktrees at once" already offers. Competitor behavior is evidence here, never a requirement.

## Target steps

Today: 5 steps, two of them blind: open **Worktrees** (focus lands in the search field at the bottom), scan rows and click badges for reasons, press **Switch and restart stack** with nothing said about what it stops or drops, watch one spinner for 14 to 27 s, land on the reloaded page with no outcome (the terminal holds it, naming folders). Proposed: 4 steps: open **Worktrees** with focus in **Find checkout**, pick a row with the arrows and Enter, press **Switch and Restart Stack** after reading its reach line, read the outcome on the reloaded page, where focus starts.

1. **Open Worktrees (`GET /__devhost__/worktrees`, read only).** The bar's **Worktrees** trigger shows the selected checkout's branch, or "Detached" with its 7-character commit ID, and its accessible name says the same, "Worktrees: main" (`SEL-4`, `WORD-1`). When a repository of the stack is blocked or its selected checkout is unavailable, the trigger adds the state as a shape and a word ("fix-nav · deleted"), never by color alone, and the collapsed badge carries the same condition with its one-line notice beside it (`ACT-13`, `SURF-8`, `ACT-7`). The trigger is one arrow stop on the bar: Alt+Ctrl+B focuses the bar, ArrowLeft and ArrowRight reach it, and Enter, Space or the arrow toward the panel opens it (`KEY-3`, `KEY-4`). The panel is an anchored popover (`SURF-2`), placed and sized so it never overlaps the notice stack in either corner placement, painted above docked chat, and fitted to every acceptance viewport with its find field, details line, reach line and footer outside the scrolling list (`SURF-1`, `SURF-9`, `SURF-10`). Opening it reads the checkout list again, as today (`git worktree list`, each checkout's manifest and service folders), and that read stops nothing (`HOLD-4`): it only publishes what it found. The panel's header names the repository, with a **Repository** choice when the stack runs services from several repositories, and says when the list was read, "Read 14:02", beside **Refresh Worktrees**; the list updates in place from the published state while the panel is open (`ACT-12`). While the list loads, its place reads "Loading checkouts of acme…" (`STATE-1`).
   - Keys: focus starts in **Find checkout**, at the top of the panel (`KEY-6`, `KEY-10`); Tab moves to the highlighted row's remedies in the details line, then the footer; Shift+Tab from the find field reaches **Refresh Worktrees** and the **Repository** choice; Tab past the last control or Shift+Tab before the first closes the panel and returns focus to the trigger (`KEY-7`); Escape or a click outside closes it, focus returning to the trigger, or, when the panel was opened with the pointer, to the page element that had focus before (`KEY-5`, `KEY-6`, `KEY-8`). Keys typed into the find field never reach the page (`KEY-5`). Under the find field, the panel's hint line reads "↑↓ Move · Enter Pick · Esc Close", drawn from the same key definitions as its handlers, in the platform's notation (`KEY-13`, `BAR-4`).
2. **Find and pick a checkout (read only).** The list is the shared checkout list, one component under `packages/devhost-ui/src/` that this flow ships in both style entries, the devtools Shadow DOM stylesheet and the status pages' document stylesheet, so the toolbar, recovery pages, the crash view and the collapsed list on an app's own 404 render the same rows with the same words (`SURF-14`, `SEL-4`). It is a combobox: the find field and the list share one shadow tree, ArrowUp and ArrowDown move the highlighted row (`aria-activedescendant`), Enter picks it, and rows are never several Tab stops and hold no controls (`KEY-10`). Rows put the main checkout first, then worktrees by branch; each row reads its label from the one checkout-label builder this flow ships with the list, "fix-nav · 08c92af · ~/acme/.worktrees/fix-nav", a long path shortened in the middle with the full path in its tooltip and accessible name (`SEL-4`, `WORD-8`), and its badges, each a shape and a word: **running**, **configured**, **unavailable**, **in use**, **blocked**, **deleted** and **edit not applied** (`ACT-13`, `WORD-6`), their words and shapes added to services-panel's shared state-words module and state indicator; `WORD-6`'s table has no words yet for in use, deleted or edit not applied, so these are proposed for it. Each badge's reason is text in the row's accessible description, never only on hover (`KEY-10`). Stack-directory cards, the running agents list, chat's conversation list and both sides of a switch in the terminal take their checkout labels from the same builder (`SEL-4`).
   - Find: branch and path match fuzzily with the matched text highlighted, and every checkout's full HEAD commit matches by exact prefix, so "08c92a" finds fix-nav and "c521449" lists both main and the detached checkout when they share that commit (`SEL-4`, `SEL-10`). A hexadecimal query that matches nothing reads "No checkout has HEAD at 08c92aff", any other query "No checkout matches "fixx"", each with **Clear Filter** (`SEL-10`, `STATE-2`). A repository with only its main checkout reads "acme has no other checkouts. Add one with git worktree add" followed by the command on its own line (`STATE-2`).
   - Defaults: the highlight and the pick start on the selected checkout when it is available; a selected checkout that was deleted or unregistered is never preselected, and the highlight then starts on the configured checkout (`SEL-4`). Enter in the find field picks the highlighted row and never starts a switch (`REV-2`, `KEY-9`).
   - The details line: below the list, outside it, headed by the highlighted row's label and its reason sentence, holding that row's remedies as the Tab stops after the find field, and updated as the highlight moves (`KEY-10`, `MENU-4`). It never covers another row's badges. For an unavailable checkout whose manifest fails, the reason is one structured line with the file relative to that checkout, its line, column and key, the path written once: "devhost.toml does not parse · line 13, services.api.port: strings cannot contain newlines", with **Open in Editor** at that line in that checkout (`MENU-4`'s **Open**, labelled as `WORD-2` names the operation), **Copy Error**, which copies the original text, and **Check Again**, which reads the list again once the file is fixed, because devhost does not watch the manifests of checkouts it does not run; validation issues list one per line (`MENU-4`, `FAIL-2`, `WORD-7`, `HOLD-1`). A worktree whose folder is gone reads "deleted · ~/acme/.worktrees/old is gone; Git still lists it", never a raw Git exit status, with the command `git worktree prune` to copy, because devhost runs only read-only Git commands (`WORD-7`, `SURF-15`).
3. **Read the reach and press Switch and Restart Stack (`POST /__devhost__/worktrees` with an `operationId`, one write).** The footer's button reads **Switch and Restart Stack** on every surface, its label carrying no count, in title-style capitalization while its reach line, reasons, outcomes and state words use sentence case (`WORD-2`, `WORD-3`, `BAR-2`, `MENU-6`). One line beside it names the stack, the repository and the target checkout and what the press does: "Restarts all 6 services of Acme Shop from fix-nav ~/acme/.worktrees/fix-nav (repository acme) with new automatic ports · took 14 s last time", and its tooltip and accessible description list the six, "web, api, docs, worker, admin and mail" (`SEL-5`, `SEL-1`, `BAR-2`, `REV-2`). In a run started with names, the line names what stays off: "Restarts web, api and docs of Acme Shop from fix-nav ~/acme/.worktrees/fix-nav with new automatic ports; worker, admin and mail stay not started" (`SEL-5`, `SEL-7`). While a manifest edit is rejected, the line adds that the switch runs the target checkout's own manifest, which it checks first (`HOLD-6`).
   - Picking is the first step and the button the second (`REV-2`): the button runs on one press when nothing else is at stake (`REV-1`), and it is unavailable, focusable with its reason as visible text in the panel's status line and as its accessible description, while nothing other than the running checkout is picked ("Pick a checkout other than main, which runs now"), while another operation runs ("Restart Stack is running · 3 of 6 started", `HOLD-3`), while the page has lost its stack's status stream (`HOLD-2`) and while the repository is blocked (step 7) (`MENU-3`, `HOLD-1`).
   - **Other work asks first, in place (`REV-3`, `REV-4`).** Before it runs, the control names each item of other work the switch moves the page away from: every chat conversation of this checkout that works or waits for the person, by agent, title and edit mode in the owner's words ("Claude Code · Fix the nav · This checkout (live) · waiting for you", `WORD-10`, `O1`), every agent the running agents list sees working in this checkout ("Codex in a terminal · working"), unsaved Configuration window drafts of this checkout's files, a held reload, and page context (`SEL-5`). While any exists, the press expands in place into one question, a group labelled by its sentence, "Switch Acme Shop to fix-nav ~/acme/.worktrees/fix-nav and leave this work in main?", described by its items and by the six services it restarts:
     - "Claude Code · Fix the nav · This checkout (live) · waiting for you: it keeps waiting in main, and its request stays answerable from every page of Acme Shop" (`SEL-5`, `REV-9`);
     - "devhost.toml: an unsaved draft for main, which the Configuration window opens again after you return to main" (`UNDO-2`);
     - "Hold Reloads is keeping this page as it is; the switch reloads it" (`REV-3`);
     - "Page context: 2 marks; a mark whose element is gone after the reload is listed as lost" (`UNDO-2`, `SEL-9`).
     Its first choice leads to the work and takes the pressed button's place with focus and Enter: **Answer First** while an agent waits for the person, which closes the panel and opens docked chat on that conversation's decision card; else **Review Drafts** while drafts are named, which opens the Configuration window on the first one; else **Cancel**, the reading this proposal follows where `REV-3` names no first choice (a working agent, a terminal agent, a held reload or page context). **Switch Anyway** sits beside it (`REV-3`, `REV-4`, `WORD-2`). A double press lands on the first choice. Escape, or a click in the panel on no control, cancels the question and returns focus to **Switch and Restart Stack** with the panel open; a click on another control cancels it and that control acts; Tab past the panel's last control, Shift+Tab before its first and a click outside close the panel and cancel the question with it (`REV-4`, `KEY-7`, `KEY-8`). With no such work the switch asks nothing, and with chat off the reach line says that agents were not checked (`STATE-2`).
   - The services runtime runs every check before it stops anything: the target is registered and available, its manifest and includes load, its service folders exist, and its hostnames and fixed ports are free, probed on their bind host with the holder named; a failed check refuses the switch with nothing stopped and names the check ("fix-nav's devhost.toml line 13 does not parse; nothing was stopped") (`REV-11`, `FAIL-2`). A target that changed since the list was read is refused with the current state, the list redraws from it and the pick is kept when it still exists ("fix-nav was deleted after the list was read at 14:02; nothing was stopped") (`REV-13`).
4. **Watch the switch in place (the operation record, read only).** The confirm control stays where it was as the busy control and keeps focus, `aria-busy` and `aria-disabled`, never `disabled`, reading what runs: "Switching to fix-nav · 3 of 6 stopped · 5 s", then "Switching to fix-nav · 2 of 6 started · 9 s" (`KEY-11`, `FB-9`, `WORD-4`). Every surface draws the switching repository's services as "switching to fix-nav ~/acme/.worktrees/fix-nav" and the target's services as starting with their health check, never failed, never running from the old checkout (`ACT-3`, `ACT-4`); the bar's trigger reads "main · switching to fix-nav" and the old row keeps **running** until the target's services run (`ACT-3`). 5. **Read the outcome on the reloaded page (read only).** The switch succeeds only once every target service passed its health check, with no leftover of a killed run answering in its place, and the routes and hot reload follow the target (`FB-5`, `ACT-11`); only then does devhost save fix-nav as the stack's selection, which the next `devhost start` restores, and remove the pending-switch record (`UNDO-1`, `worktree-switch-15`), and only then does the page reload at the same URL. The trigger names fix-nav (`SURF-15`). Its previous checkout, "main ~/acme", is a link that opens **Worktrees** with main highlighted, so the way back is one switch away (`UNDO-5`). A success closes 4 s after the pointer and focus leave it and stays readable in the stack's log, where the link stays too (`FB-6`, `FB-7`, `O14`). Chat keeps its agent, model, edit mode, open state, side and draft across the switch, because they belong to the stack and page origin, not the checkout (`SEL-11`, `CC-9`).
   - When the page the switch reloaded is answered with a 5xx by a target service whose health check passed, the outcome says so instead of a plain success, "Switched acme to fix-nav ~/acme/.worktrees/fix-nav: 6 of 6 running · web answered 500 for this page", and the app's answer shows with devhost's notice (`SURF-16`, `FB-5`); when web's last output names a missing dependency, that notice leads with `MENU-5`'s needs-setup action: "web in fix-nav could not resolve "react"" with the command to run there, `bun install`, to copy, then **Restart** (`O32`, `FAIL-3`).
   - The terminal prints one line when the switch starts and one outcome line, naming both checkouts by label: "Switching acme from main ~/acme to fix-nav ~/acme/.worktrees/fix-nav · restarting 6 services", then "Switched acme from main ~/acme to fix-nav ~/acme/.worktrees/fix-nav: restarted 6 services · 6 of 6 running · 14 s", and no "exited … waiting for a restart" line for any service the switch stopped (`FB-10`, `FAIL-5`, `WORD-4`, `SEL-4`). Agent output (`AGENT=1`) carries the same facts with absolute paths, a failure on one `devhost: ERR:` line (`FB-11`, `WORD-9`), and `devhost status` reports the switch as the stack's last operation (`FB-11`, `ACT-1`).
   - A switch has no terminal command of its own: the terminal reports switches the page starts, and the way to run another checkout from a terminal is to start that checkout's own stack in its folder, as "Several worktrees at once" documents. This is a proposed exception to `MENU-6` until the reference names a command; stack-run-stop owns terminal commands. Stop Stack's terminal counterpart exists: `devhost stop` in that checkout's folder, which resolves its stack per `SEL-6`.
6. **Recover from a failed switch, in place (one write per retry).** When a target service cannot start, devhost stops the target's services and starts the previous checkout's again service by service, with its routes and hot reload; the saved selection never left main, because a switch saves its target only once it succeeds, and the pending-switch record is removed (Backend 13); while it restores, the busy control and the touched rows read "Returning to main ~/acme" (`UNDO-1`, `ACT-3`). Returned to main ~/acme: 6 of 6 running · 21 s", with web's evidence from the attempt, its exit code with the time, its last output lines frozen at the exit, the probe it failed and what devhost ran ("Ran bun run dev in web/ · fix-nav's devhost.toml line 9") (`FAIL-1`, `FAIL-2`, `FAIL-3`, `FB-2`, `FB-9`). The page does not reload.    - A rejected manifest edit never makes its checkout unavailable (`HOLD-6`, `HOLD-4`): with an edit on disk that hot reload rejected, the read keeps the running checkout **running**, adds **edit not applied**, and the details line gives the rejection once, "devhost.toml line 12, column 3, services.worker: unrecognized key "dependOn" is not applied; the stack still runs the accepted manifest", with **Edit** (the Configuration window at that line) or, with the config editor off, **Open in Editor** and **Copy Error** (`MENU-4`); nothing is stopped, blocked or disabled, and switching away stays possible.

## Backend and contract changes

**Backend** (the services runtime in `apps/devhost/internal/services`, the control server in `apps/devhost/internal/devtools` and the stack records in `apps/devhost/internal/caddy`, preserving the owner rule that a switch never falls back silently, validation before any stop, the rollback to the previous checkout, one operation at a time per stack, read-only Git, the identity checks before every signal and every page's status stream):

1. **Keep the run's selection.** The switch passes the run's requested names (`requested: r.manifest.requested` in its `configurationChange`, `apps/devhost/internal/services/runtime.go:398`), the switch's preparation resolves the target with them (`apps/devhost/internal/services/worktrees.go:355`), and `restore` resolves a saved linked worktree with the names `devhost start` resolved (`apps/devhost/internal/services/worktrees.go:297`); the helper that resolves a checkout's manifest takes the requested list as a required argument, so no caller can drop it again. A requested name the target does not define is published in the switch's record as `missingNames` and printed. Serves `worktree-switch-11`, `worktree-switch-12`. Required. Contract: none; `guides/stack-lifecycle.md` already promises it.
2. **A read stops nothing.** `refreshWorktrees` judges the running checkout only by whether its folder exists, Git still lists it and it belongs to the repository; a manifest problem of the running checkout stays hot reload's and is published on the running entry as `notApplied` with the structured error, never as unavailable. A deleted or unregistered running checkout publishes the repository as `blocked` with `blockedKind` (`deleted` or `unregistered`) and its reason sentence, stops no process and starts none of the repository's services until the person switches or the folder loads again; services that exit meanwhile stay blocked; tool launches stay refused. Serves `worktree-switch-14`, `worktree-switch-05`, `network-exposure-03` (the read's side effect). Required. Contract: **[discuss]** below (`HOLD-4`).
3. **Structured checkout entries.** Each checkout in `repositories[].worktrees` carries `state` (`available`, `unavailable`, `in-use`, `blocked`, `deleted`, `unregistered`), `branch`, the full `head` and `shortHead`, `configured`, `selected`, `running`, `notApplied`, and `reason` as `{kind, file, line, column, key, message, issues[], original}` from manifest-hot-reload's structured error (`MENU-4`), the file relative to that checkout; an in-use entry's owner adds its checkout label, `startedAt` and how it was started (`foreground` or `detached`); the response carries `readAt`. Serves `worktree-switch-02`, `worktree-switch-03`, `worktree-switch-07`. Required.
4. **The switch's operation record.** Both entry points' `POST` takes `operationId` and answers `202 Accepted` with the record before the switch runs: `kind` `switch` (or `return` for Return to Configured Checkout), `repositoryId`, `from` and `to` checkout labels, `origin`, the target services, each one's `step` (`stopping`, `starting`, `health`, `running`, `failed`) with its health check's `elapsedMs` and `timeoutMs`, the switch's own `step` (`check`, `stop`, `start`, `routes`, `accept`, `restore`), `missingNames`, `leftBehind` (the conversations and agents of the old checkout that work or wait, from agent-chat's session list and the running agents scan), `outcome` and the times, streamed on `/__devhost__/ws/health` at least once a second; a repeated ID returns the record. A switch while another operation runs answers `409` with `{error}` naming it; a target that changed since it was read answers `409` with the current state; an unknown repository or checkout answers `422`; none answers plain text. The last switch's duration feeds the reach line. Serves `worktree-switch-09`, `worktree-switch-04`, `chat-checkout-switch-02`, `worktree-switch-06`. Required. Contract: `CC-37`.
5. **Per-service rollback with its own outcome.** A target service that cannot start makes the switch restore the previous checkout service by service with its routes, watcher inputs and saved selection, publishing `restoring`; a service that cannot come back is left failed with its recovery page while the rest run; the outcome names what was restored. Serves `worktree-switch-06`. Required. (`UNDO-1`.)
6. **The stream stays open; the page reloads after the outcome.** The control server keeps every page's status stream open through a switch, and the switch's success is published only after every target service passed its health check, so the page reloads once, after the outcome. Serves `worktree-switch-09`. Required. Contract: `CC-26`'s stream rule, which services-panel builds.
7. **The switch's terminal lines.** `cliout` prints one start line and one outcome line per switch with both checkouts' labels, in human and agent output; services the switch stops are marked as devhost's own stops, so the exit watcher prints nothing for them; a start that restores a saved checkout other than the configured one names it in its ready line. Serves `worktree-switch-04`, `worktree-switch-12`. Required. Contract: `CC-22`, `CC-24`.
8. **Stop Stack through the shared stop routine.** `stopWorktreeOccupant` (`apps/devhost/internal/services/worktree_ownership.go`) calls the one stop routine stack-run-stop builds for `devhost stop`, with `O30`'s allowance (a minute plus 20 s per service); it never sends SIGKILL to a supervisor while that stack's services are alive, publishes the other stack's progress (`stopped` of `total`) under the switch's kind `stop-other`, and answers survivors with `{service, pid, port}`, keeping the checkout in use; before its first signal it writes why beside the other stack's record ("from Acme Shop (main ~/acme)'s Worktrees panel"), which that stack prints when it stops (`ACT-10`). The route takes `operationId` like a switch. Serves `worktree-switch-13`, `worktree-switch-07`. Required. Contract: `CC-17`, `CC-35`.
9. **[discuss]: Needs setup from the failing service's output.** When a target service exits during a switch, or the page the switch reloaded is answered with a 5xx by a target service, the record carries that service's last output lines (`FAIL-3`), and when they match a missing-dependency message of a known runtime (Bun, Node, Python, Ruby) devhost names the cause and the command to run in that checkout from the lockfile it finds (`bun.lock`: `bun install`). `O32` sets the behavior but no backend contract: the backend owners must define and calibrate the matchers, and the reference's author must give `MENU-5`'s needs-setup row its published cause. Serves `worktree-switch-06`. Required.
10. **The origin of switches and stops.** Each switch, return and stop records its origin as `ACT-1` defines it (this tab or another by page ID, the terminal, an agent), and the acting stack's terminal line ends with it. Serves `network-exposure-03`, `worktree-switch-07`. Required.
11. **[discuss]: Who may switch, stop or read checkouts from the network.** `network-exposure-03`'s proposal, carried here as the recommended default until the product owner decides with `network-exposure-01`'s stopgap: both entry points of the worktree handler (`/__devhost__/worktrees` and a service hostname with `X-Devhost-Recovery: worktrees`) call the config save's `isLocalClient` check for `GET` and `POST`, answer any other client `403` with `{"error": "Checkouts can be switched only from this machine."}` and run nothing for it; the recovery entry point also applies the `Host` rule of that service's hostnames; the health stream sent to such a client leaves out checkout paths, owner PIDs and owner IDs; the acting stack's terminal names a refused client's address; an off-by-default setting beside `stackDirectoryAllowRemote` in the devhost config file restores remote use. The reference's section 1 leaves who can reach the control API to the product owner, so the product owner must settle this rule, and `restart-service`, `restart-stack` and `start-service`, which share the gap, follow the same decision. Serves `network-exposure-03`. Required once decided.
12. **Check Again needs no new route.** A blocked repository's **Check Again** is the checkout-list read **Refresh Worktrees** makes (`GET /__devhost__/worktrees`), which Backend 2 makes stop nothing; services-panel's group header and recovery pages call the same read. Serves `worktree-switch-05`. Required.
13. **Save the selection once the switch succeeds.** `prepare` (`apps/devhost/internal/services/worktrees.go:360`) writes a pending-switch record beside the stack's `active.json` instead of the selection: `repositoryId`, the checkout left (`from`), the target (`to`), the switch's `operationId` and its start time, with the same atomic write, so a state directory that cannot be written still refuses the switch before anything stops. `switchWorktreeLocked` writes `active.json` only after `applyConfiguration` returns nil, then removes the record; on an error or a cancellation it removes the record and leaves `active.json` as it was, so the restore write (`apps/devhost/internal/services/runtime.go:399-400`) has nothing left to undo. At `devhost start` (`apps/devhost/internal/services/stack.go:145-150`), a record whose target differs from `active.json` means the switch never finished: the start runs the checkout `active.json` names, prints the unfinished switch once in human and agent output before its ready line, publishes that switch's record under its `operationId` with the outcome `unfinished` for a page that reconnects, and removes the record; a record whose target equals `active.json` belongs to a switch that finished and is only removed. Backend 8 and `O30` remove devhost's own forced kill but not crashes, so this is needed as well. Serves `worktree-switch-15`. Required. Contract: **[discuss]** below.

## Recommended priority

**P1.** The realistic trigger is routine: deleting a finished worktree after a merge, by hand, with a cleanup script or by an agent, while the stack still runs it, lands every page on a recovery state whose prominent **Restart** cannot work and whose list preselects the deleted checkout (`worktree-switch-05`); and saving a manifest edit that hot reload rejects, then opening **Worktrees** before fixing it, stops every service of the repository, which in a single-repository project is the whole stack, right after devhost said the edit changed nothing, with **Retry** disabled and the fix on disk restoring nothing (`worktree-switch-14`). Both are recoverable, but only through a control the page does not lead with, and both cost the whole stack's uptime. Every switch also throws away a run's service selection, starting workers and indexers people left off (`worktree-switch-11`, `worktree-switch-12`), hides a waiting agent without a word (`chat-checkout-switch-02`), and **Stop Stack** ends a parallel stack, often another agent's, with one click and can leave its services running unseen (`worktree-switch-07`, `worktree-switch-13`). No files are lost: a switch restarts processes and runs only read-only Git, so the cost is downtime, side effects of unwanted services and lost trust, not data. Switching is less frequent than restarting a service from the Services panel, but it is the review step the agent edit mode's **New worktree (review before merge)** leads to (`UNDO-5`), so it grows with agent use. Ship first the slice with no new backend contract: the shared checkout list with its keyboard model, commit-prefix find and label builder; the deleted state led by **Return to Configured Checkout** on every surface, never preselecting the deleted checkout, together with the read that stops nothing and the running checkout judged only by its folder, so no release ever leaves a deleted checkout's services running without saying so; the kept selection on switch and restore; the reach line; the questions for other work, **Stop Stack** and **Return to Configured Checkout**; same-tab links. Then the switch's operation record with its progress, its outcome on the reloaded page, the per-service rollback, the selection saved only once a switch succeeds with an unfinished switch named at the next start (`worktree-switch-15`), and the terminal lines, built on services-panel's operation record. Then **Stop Stack** through stack-run-stop's shared stop routine with attribution, once that routine exists, the structured reasons once manifest-hot-reload's structured error exists, the needs-setup matchers once their contract is settled, and the network rule once the product owner decides it. This flow ships the shared checkout list and the checkout-label builder that stack-directory, services-panel, startup-and-recovery, running-agents and agent-chat reuse (`SURF-14`, `SEL-4`). It depends on the foundations notices-and-feedback (the notice stack, the announcer, activity lines and outcome notices on the reloaded page), services-panel (the published service state with `blocked` and switching rows, the operation record, the shared state indicator) and toolbar-shell (the bar's arrow stops, the collapsed badge, `Button`'s unavailable state). Seams, with no blocker in either direction: agent-chat supplies the sessions of every checkout (`CC-38`) and the per-stack chat settings (`CC-9`) the question and outcome read, and its worktree-mode report opens this panel (`UNDO-5`); running-agents supplies the terminal agents the question names; config-editor supplies the drafts the question names and states a save's return to the configured checkout; reload-control supplies the held reload; page-context restores marks after the reload (`UNDO-2`); startup-and-recovery hosts the list in its recovery view, crash view and app 404 notice; stack-run-stop owns the shared stop routine, `devhost status` and the ready line; manifest-hot-reload owns the structured error, the external-service block's reload line and the rejection notice that clears with the deleted checkout.
