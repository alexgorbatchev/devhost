Watch and restart services from the toolbar

TLDR: Every service of the stack is counted and listed with its state, cause and route; each restart or start shows its steps and its outcome on the row that started it, with focus kept there; command-mode serve sessions model their command first-class with Restart Command; narrow popovers wrap cleanly clear of the minimap with >=3:1 button borders; and Restart Stack sits apart from Restart Changed and asks first, in place.

Low-fidelity wireframe. Layout, names and numbers are illustrative; the redesign ticket's acceptance criteria are authoritative.

How to read: Toast: passing status message in a corner, links only, no decisions Popover or menu: opens from the outlined control its arrow points at Panel: docked beside the table, stays open Dimmed backdrop: dialog that blocks the page Selected rows Just changed Row cursor 1Callout, explained on the right

1. Read the stack at a glance

The Services trigger keeps its place on the bar and still opens the Services panel. What changes is what it counts and how it draws: it reads the published state of every service of the stack (matching devhost stack info / devhost status, O62), never a guess of its own, and tells each state by shape and text (ACT-2, ACT-13). Command-mode devhost serve -- <cmd> sessions count only the command itself (1/1; step 7). Condition notices, such as api's failure notice, belong to notices-and-feedback; this page draws them only beside the open panel in step 2 and in their one-line form beside the collapsed badge. Keys: Alt+Ctrl+B focuses the bar; the trigger is an arrow stop (ArrowLeft, ArrowRight, Home, End); Enter, Space or the arrow toward the panel opens it (KEY-3, KEY-4).

  1. 1 One dot per service while the dots fit on the bar's row, decided by a fit test at every window width, never by a service count. Every service of the stack is counted, the 2 the run left out as hollow dots, and the states that need the person come first: ▲ failed, ● running, ○ not started, in forced colors too. The trigger's accessible name spells it out: "Services: 3 of 6 running, 1 failed: api, 2 not started, 2 changed" (BAR-1, SURF-10, ACT-6, ACT-13, WORD-5).
  2. 2 The changed badge and its restart icon stay beside the summary while a failure shows, because changed and failed are independent; today the badge disappears whenever any error shows (SEL-2, ACT-8).
  3. 3 When the dots would wrap the bar, a fixed-width summary takes their place, no wider with 40 services than with 8: counts by state, up to three services that need the person, then "+N". Its accessible name reads "Services: 38 of 40 running, 2 failed: flood, s27" (SURF-10, ACT-13, WORD-5).
  4. 4 A service that is starting counts as starting, with its own shape, and never takes the failed look while its health check runs (ACT-4, STATE-1).
  5. 5 Collapsed, the badge takes the look of the highest condition, here api's failure, and still names the changed services; the failure's notice stays beside it in one line. A routine restart, of one row or of the whole stack, never raises the badge (ACT-7, SURF-8).
  6. 6 Every dot, row and state word comes from one shared service state indicator (shape, word and accessible name) and one shared module of state words, shipped in the devtools stylesheet and in the status pages' stylesheet, so api's status page and the stack directory's cards draw "failed" with the same ▲ and the same words as the bar and the panel (SURF-14, ACT-13, WORD-6).

2. Open the Services panel

The trigger still opens the panel, and the panel still lists the stack's services with Restart on each, as today. What changes is that it lists every service, the ones the run left out included, says where each runs and why it is not running, keeps its actions within reach and clear of the minimap at any viewport width, and uses >=3:1 button borders (--dh-input-line) in both themes. Keys: Tab and Shift+Tab move through the filter, each row's actions and the footer in visual order; Tab past the last control or Shift+Tab before the first closes the panel and returns focus to the trigger (KEY-7); Escape or a click outside the panel closes it and returns focus to the trigger, or, when the panel was opened with the pointer, to the page element that had focus before (KEY-5, KEY-6, KEY-8).

  1. 1 The panel opens anchored to the trigger and keeps the page, its scroll position and the person's place. Its first line is the stack's summary sentence, and no heading inside it repeats "Services" (SURF-2, ACT-6, MENU-2, serve-command-09).
  2. 2 Rows are grouped by the manifest's group or include file, and by repository first when the stack has more than one; each header carries its count and its state, and every group starts expanded. Unrouted services without manifest groups use a distinct heading (such as Background) or omit the subsection heading when only one group or a command session is shown (MENU-2, SURF-10, serve-command-09).
  3. 3 Whenever a service is not plainly running, its row says so in one word with its cause and time. Every row shows its route as text and its port, and a "changed" tag names the changed files. A name links only to an address that answers and opens it in the same tab, as every devhost link does except Watch Live Activity (today it opens a new tab; editor integration is removed under O51): api's name opens its health URL /api/hello, never devhost's 404 page; worker has no route, so its name is plain text. Each row's shape, word and accessible name come from the shared state indicator (ACT-2, WORD-6, WORD-8, SURF-14, SURF-15).
  4. 4 Row actions are Tab stops in row order, each named with its target ("Restart web", "Logs web"): Restart on a managed service, Start on a not-started one, Logs, and Edit on a failed or exited one. Opened from the keyboard while the rows fit, the panel puts focus on the first row action. Restart and Start match the terminal's devhost service restart web and devhost service start admin (KEY-6, KEY-10, WORD-2, MENU-6, CC-11).
  5. 5 The footer keeps Restart 2 Changed and Start 2 Services together, and Restart Stack at the far end past a separator, in another button style; steps 5, 6 and 7 show each (BAR-2, REV-2).
  6. 6 A blocked repository says once why and offers its ways out, led by returning to the configured worktree: links to Return to Configured Worktree and to choosing another worktree in the Worktrees panel, which worktree-switch owns, and Check Again, which reads the worktree list again and clears the block once the worktree loads, stopping nothing. Its services that do not run read "blocked", and every Restart of the repository is unavailable with that sentence as its accessible description, never drawn as failed (HOLD-4, HOLD-1, MENU-5, MENU-3, ACT-2, WORD-11).
  7. 7 External services show what devhost checks, the result and when, and that they run outside devhost, with no Restart, Start, Logs or changed tag; an unreachable one leads with Check Again, which runs the check now and updates the row's result and time; the panel says once that devhost checks them but cannot restart them. External services never block a repository's worktree switch and need no warning when managed services switch: they stay untouched and marked external (ACT-9, MENU-5, HOLD-1, MENU-3, SEL-5, O7).
  8. 8 When the rows overflow, a filter appears and takes focus on open. It matches name, hostname and group as the person types and keeps group headers with their counts: "s34" leaves only s34's row under its group header (SEL-10, SURF-10, KEY-6).
  9. 9 Rows that need the person (failed, exited, blocked, changed) are pinned in a group above the rest. Only the rows scroll: the summary, the filter and the footer stay in view at every acceptance viewport (SURF-10, SURF-9).
  10. 10 A filter with no match says what was searched, with Clear Filter (SEL-10, STATE-2).
  11. 11 From the moment the status stream closes, every row keeps its last known state drawn as unknown with the time it was last seen, never down; the trigger shows the connection's state instead of a count, and every write is unavailable with the one reason the backend sent, said once in the panel's status line and given to each write as its accessible description: here, after devhost stop, the stopped stack's one sentence with its time, reason and start command. Notices-and-feedback owns the lost-connection notice and Retry Now (ACT-5, ACT-10, HOLD-1, HOLD-2, MENU-3, CC-26).
  12. 12 The notice stack sits between the bar and every other surface, and the open panel is placed and sized so it never covers a notice, in either corner and with chat docked: api's failure notice and its Logs stay readable and clickable while the panel is open (SURF-1, SURF-9).
  13. 13 At 320 px and 390 px (and desktop widths), ToolbarPopover reserves --devhost-minimap-collapsed-width so the open popover clears the 12 px right-edge log minimap strip, RestartStackButton uses flex-wrap and hides the desktop keyboard shortcut pill on narrow viewports so Restart 2 Changed never clips, the unrouted subsection reads Background instead of repeating Services, and default devtools buttons use --dh-input-line borders meeting >=3:1 contrast in both dark and light themes (SURF-9, MENU-2, ACT-13, serve-command-09).
  14. 14 From another environment, browsers signed in with the shared-domain passkey (with phone support) or paired via devhost pair use every write identically to this machine; an unauthenticated remote browser sees writes and Check Again unavailable with "Sign in with passkey or pair this browser to use devhost here", and the control server answers 403 with no worktree paths or PIDs (HOLD-7, HOLD-8, O36, O37).

3. Restart one service

Restart on a row still restarts one service, keeps its automatic port, and succeeds only once the service passes its health check with its routes updated, as today. What changes is that the row shows each step, other restarts wait in a visible queue instead of on a hidden lock, and focus stays on the button. Keys: Enter or Space on Restart starts it and focus stays on it while it runs; Cancel Restart on a queued row is an ordinary Tab stop named with its target, "Cancel Restart of worker" (KEY-10, KEY-11).

  1. 1 Restart runs on one press, because restarting one named service is not disruptive. The pressed button keeps focus and reads "Restarting api…", aria-busy and aria-disabled but never disabled, so focus never drops to the page (REV-1, KEY-11, FB-9).
  2. 2 The row shows each step with its elapsed time, updated at least once a second: queued, stopping, starting, "health check 2 s of 30 s", then running. Every other row keeps its own state. The route answers 202 Accepted with the operation's record before the restart runs (FB-1, ACT-3, STATE-1, ACT-1).
  3. 3 A Restart of another service queues behind the running one, or behind a hot reload, instead of being locked out: worker reads "queued behind Restart api" with Cancel Restart on every open tab, which takes it out of the queue and reports "Cancelled the queued restart of worker", and it keeps its place through a page reload. A second Restart of api is unavailable with "api is already restarting"; while the stack starts or shuts down, every Restart and Start is unavailable with that operation and its progress, "Starting Acme Shop · 2 of 6 started" (SEL-7, WORD-2, HOLD-3, CC-8, REV-12, WORD-7).
  4. 4 Restart Stack, a stack-wide write, is unavailable while any other operation runs, with that operation and its progress beside it, which is also its accessible description (HOLD-3, HOLD-1, MENU-3).
  5. 5 A daemon whose stop command is slow shows it on its row with the elapsed time, and the rows queued behind it say what they wait for (FAIL-3, HOLD-3).
  6. 6 Under O41, a daemon's status read and the stop command it leads to share one 20 s deadline counted from the status read's start (overridable per service via [services.<name>.lifecycle].stopTimeout). At expiry, devhost kills the command's whole process group (sending SIGKILL to -pgid even if the group leader exited early, fixing manifest-hot-reload-2-06, and logging killed leftovers), treats a cut status read as "did not finish" rather than "not running", reports one sentence with a fresh status read afterwards, ends the operation failed for mail, and lets the queue, manifest saves and Ctrl-C proceed; during stack shutdown (O30), daemon and foreground stops run in parallel from t = 0 inside the configurable 30 s default stopTimeout, widening automatically when a daemon override exceeds 20 s unless CLI --timeout caps the run (Backend 4; CC-35).

4. Read the outcome on the row, or recover in place

A restart still ends with the service running or not, as today; what changes is that the row says which, why and what to do next, and keeps saying it until the person deals with it. A response devhost cannot read, such as the proxy's HTML page, becomes one sentence naming what is not running and the command that starts it, with the original text behind Copy Error, never markup (FAIL-2). Keys: focus stays on Restart; Tab reaches Logs, Edit and Dismiss in order (KEY-10, KEY-11).

  1. 1 Success replaces the busy state on the row, "Restarted api · 2.6 s", announced once in the polite region while focus stays on Restart; it stays while the panel is open. With the panel closed, the same outcome is one notice instead, never both (FB-2, FB-3, FB-6, FB-9, WORD-4).
  2. 2 After a restart, a service's row keeps its last exit until the stack stops: "last exit: terminated by SIGTERM (143) at 21:53:34, not sent by devhost" (FAIL-3, FAIL-5, WORD-8).
  3. 3 A failure turns the row exited or failed with one cause sentence and its evidence: its own last output lines frozen at the exit, the health check's probe with the status it got, or the program that holds a fixed port, and what devhost ran: the command, its folder when it differs from the stack's, and the manifest file and line. It is announced once in the assertive region, and the sentence becomes Restart's accessible description (FAIL-2, FAIL-3, FB-9, WORD-7).
  4. 4 The row leads with the action for its cause: Restart, which keeps focus, then Logs and Edit; a retry that fails the same way updates the row's one report in place, "api exited with code 1 · 2 times, last at 14:05", and Edit then leads (FAIL-4). Edit opens the Configuration window at api's definition with the exit and last lines in its banner; with the config editor off, the row shows devhost.toml line 14 with Copy only (no editor integration, O51) (MENU-5, MENU-4, SURF-4, KEY-11).
  5. 5 The failure stays on the row until the person dismisses it with Dismiss (named "Dismiss api's failure") or the same restart succeeds; health messages, reconnects and closing the panel never remove it, and a closed panel leaves it as a failure notice (FB-6, FB-2).
  6. 6 A service killed from outside names the signal with the status a shell reports for it, its time and that devhost did not send it, with its last lines; a service devhost stops for an operation reads as that operation's step, never as an exit (FAIL-3, FAIL-5, WORD-7).
  7. 7 Logs on a row opens the pinned log panel filtered to that service, with focus in it, at the end of its output; it is one more way into the panel that log-minimap owns, beside its own entry points (SURF-13, KEY-12).

5. Restart the changed services

Restart Changed, its badge icon, a chat turn summary's Restart Changed (O58) and Alt+Ctrl+R share one target selector, and the key still never fires while focus is in a text field. What changes is that each names its set before it runs, wraps cleanly on narrow viewports with the shortcut pill hidden (serve-command-09), and reports what it did. Keys: Alt+Ctrl+R (restartServices, configurable), suspended in text entry and while a devhost menu or modal is open (KEY-1, KEY-2).

  1. 1 The footer's button names its set, "Restart 2 Changed", with the members in its tooltip and accessible description and its key hint from the binding's one definition: ⌃⌥R on macOS, Ctrl+Alt+R elsewhere. On narrow viewports, the footer wraps and hides the desktop shortcut pill so the button never clips (SEL-1, BAR-2, BAR-4, KEY-13, WORD-2, serve-command-09).
  2. 2 A service is changed while its watched files differ from what its running process launched with, so undoing an edit, for example with git checkout, clears the mark within a second without a restart, and a file saved during a restart marks the service again only if it differs from what the new process launched with. Only services devhost can restart are ever marked: watch on an external service is ignored with a warning, so Restart Changed never sends one (ACT-8, CC-25).
  3. 3 The badge's icon, a chat turn summary's Restart Changed ("api runs the old code until it restarts", O58) and Alt+Ctrl+R restart the same set, read from one selector. With the panel closed, progress is one activity line, then one outcome notice, "Restarted api and web · 4.1 s", which stays for its reading time (1.5 s plus 0.3 s per word, 4 s to 15 s — 4 s here) once the pointer and focus leave it while the page is visible, pauses on arrival when the browser window is unfocused (reading document.hasFocus()) until focus returns, and stays readable in the stack's log; with the panel open, both show on the rows instead (SEL-1, FB-1, FB-2, FB-6, FB-7, O14, O53, O58).
  4. 4 With nothing changed, the button names its fallback, the page's own service, else the stack's primary service: on the docs page it reads "Restart docs", and Alt+Ctrl+R restarts docs, never web (SEL-2, SEL-3, O4, CC-6).
  5. 5 While the stack starts, shuts down, restarts or a repository switches, the button and the badge's icon are unavailable with that operation and its progress beside them, and Alt+Ctrl+R shows that reason in a notice instead of doing nothing; while only another service restarts or a hot reload runs, the set queues behind it (SEL-2, MENU-3, HOLD-3, SEL-7, CC-8).
  6. 6 While the page's own modal dialog is open, Alt+Ctrl+R still acts: the notice stack and the announcer move into the dialog, which shows the outcome, and the rest of the toolbar shows its paused state with its reason in one line there. A Services panel that was open stays anchored and paused with the bar, inert with its writes unavailable, and is live again, its rows read from the published state, when the dialog closes (SURF-11, KEY-1, KEY-7, FB-9).

It restarts services inside the running stack (CC-11 covers per-service and group devhost service start|stop|restart <service|group>… via stack-run-stop, and devhost service restart --all is Restart Stack's terminal form, whose help says "Restart Stack in the browser", owner item q86a1; MENU-6), distinct from shipped devhost stack restart (project-scoped, a new run of the stack inside the devhost supervisor) and devhost restart, and from the new devhost upgrade; those last two replace the running devhost supervisor process (O18). What changes in the Services panel is that it sits apart from Restart Changed and asks first, in place; in the crash view and on recovery pages it still runs on one press, with the same reach in a line beside its button; and in manifest-free command-mode serve sessions it gives way to a working Restart Command. While any other operation runs, it is unavailable with that operation and its progress named (HOLD-3). Keys: Enter in the question takes Cancel; Escape cancels (KEY-9, KEY-8).
