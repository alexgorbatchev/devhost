1. Open a page: the dock mounts where this person left it

devhost still injects its toolbar into every HTML page of the stack, inside a Shadow DOM in the page's top layer, and /__devhost__/config.json is still read with no-store, so a reload always sees current settings. What changes: the response carries this person's layout record, the readouts alone are enough to inject the toolbar, and only what devhost draws takes the page's clicks. Defaults: bottom right and expanded unless the manifest or the person's layout says otherwise; Hold Reloads on, as today.

  1. 1 The dock, the bar and its notice stack together, mounts at the placement in the layout record: here the manifest's bottom right, until this person moves it (step 7). It is never drawn first somewhere else, and it is one landmark named "devhost" (SURF-7, KEY-3, CC-5).
  2. 2 Only the visible bar, each notice card, open panels and docked surfaces take pointer input. The dock's own box takes none, so the app's own [ Add to cart ] beside the notice takes the click, and Select Elements marks it; today an invisible strip there swallows both (SURF-11, SEL-9).
  3. 3 Every bar item names what it reaches in its tooltip and accessible description (SEL-3).
  4. 4 The bar renders whenever any segment has something to show: with only the readouts on, it shows CPU, RAM, Disk and Hold Reloads, and the reload hold keeps holding. Turning a feature off removes its segment and nothing else; a segment with nothing to show right now keeps its place, as Running agents does with "No agents in this checkout" (SEL-11, STATE-2).
  5. 5 A save that turns toolbar features off applies to open pages in place, asks nothing, and its outcome line names each toolbar change, never only "configuration reloaded". [devtools].enabled = false is the one master switch: new page loads get no script, open pages remove the toolbar and stop holding reloads, and the line says both (SEL-11, REV-10, FB-3).

2. Reach the dock from the keyboard

Every bar item and notice keeps its place and its pointer behavior. What changes: one chord reaches the dock from anywhere, and the dock is one landmark whose bar is one Tab stop instead of the page's last dozen. Keys: Alt+Ctrl+B; ArrowLeft, ArrowRight, Home and End on the bar; Enter, Space, or ArrowUp (dock at the bottom) or ArrowDown (dock at the top) opens an item's panel or menu; Tab and Shift+Tab leave the bar (KEY-3, KEY-4).

  1. 1 The notice stack and the bar form one complementary landmark named "devhost". Its DOM and Tab order follow its drawn order: with the dock at the bottom, each notice's actions come first, then the bar, then the pinned log panel and docked chat (KEY-3).
  2. 2 Alt+Ctrl+B moves focus from anywhere on the page to the bar item focused last, else the stack name (KEY-3).
  3. 3 The bar is one Tab stop built on the toolbar pattern: the arrow keys walk every item, each readout included, and Tab leaves it (KEY-4).
  4. 4 An unavailable item stays in place as an arrow stop, aria-disabled, its reason as its accessible description: here Restart Changed's icon while "Restart Stack is running · 3 of 6 started", said once as visible text in the notice stack, the dock's status line. This flow ships Button's unavailable state for every flow; a focused control that becomes unavailable keeps focus (MENU-3, HOLD-1, HOLD-3, KEY-11).
  5. 5 Hints come from one binding registry, shown in the platform's notation and outside the accessible name (KEY-13, BAR-4).
  6. 6 Pressed again, the chord moves on to the pinned log panel while it is open, then to docked chat's composer, and from the last of these back to the element that had focus before the first press (KEY-3).
  7. 7 With the dock at the top, the notices are drawn below the bar, so the bar comes first in DOM and Tab order (KEY-3).

3. Read the bar and its readouts

The bar still reads, from its start: the stack name, Services, Worktrees, CPU, RAM and Disk, Config, the launchers, Running agents, Hold Reloads and Chat, and the readouts still measure the whole machine and hide while their stream is down. Each segment is its own flow's; this flow owns the bar they sit in: its order, its fit, its keyboard model and its shared looks. Keys: each readout is an arrow stop; Enter or Space opens its details with focus on their first line; Escape closes them and returns focus to the readout (KEY-4, KEY-6, KEY-8).

  1. 1 The order stays as today. The changed badge's restart icon and Alt+Ctrl+R act on the set Restart Changed names (SEL-1, SEL-2); Config's tooltip reads "Edit this stack's configuration: its manifest files and the devhost config file" (WORD-1).
  2. 2 Each readout is a bar item button labelled by its figure. Its level follows a steadied reading, never one 2 s sample: CPU averages the last 10 s, high from 70% (back below 65%), very high from 90% held for 10 s (back below 85%); Disk reads free space, low below 10% free, very low below 5% or 10 GiB. A level shows its word and a mark of its own, ▴ ▴▴ ▾ ▾▾, never a service state's glyph or the failed look (ACT-13).
  3. 3 Its details, a popover anchored to it, give the machine's figures, this stack's share with its top three services, the other programs, the last 60 s with their lowest, highest and average as text, and one verdict sentence. Sizes use binary units with their names (WORD-8, SURF-2, KEY-12).
  4. 4 The bar keeps one row at every acceptance viewport by a fit test at the current width, never a count, in this order until its items fit; the Services summary is services-panel's form and Devtools third-party-devtools' (SURF-9, SURF-10, BAR-1).
  5. 5 More sits at the bar's end; each item in it keeps its label, state and binding. At 640×400 CSS pixels the bar shows the summary, Devtools and Host, every item stays reachable and every pointer target is at least 24 by 24 CSS pixels (SURF-9, O8).
  6. 6 In forced colors every state keeps a shape and a word: Hold Reloads pressed against unpressed, each readout level, available against unavailable items, a failure notice against a neutral one, the badge's looks (step 8) and the focus ring; forced-color-adjust: none applies only to state glyphs (ACT-13).

4. Open a panel, a menu or chat without covering a notice

Panels stay native popovers anchored to their trigger, one at a time, dismissed by Escape, a click outside or Tab past their last control; docked chat and the Configuration window keep their places and their behavior. What changes: the dock publishes one inset, the distance from its edge to the far edge of the notice stack, and every surface reads it, so nothing devhost draws covers a notice. The notices' order, count and "N more" are notices-and-feedback's; their place is this flow's. Keys: on open, focus moves to a panel's filter when it has one, else its first control; Tab past the last control or Shift+Tab before the first closes the panel and returns focus to its trigger; Escape closes only the innermost layer and never collapses the toolbar, dismisses a notice or answers anything (KEY-6, KEY-7, KEY-8).

  1. 1 The notice stack sits beside the bar, between it and every other devhost surface, in every placement, and its buttons take their own clicks with any panel or chat open (SURF-1, FB-8).
  2. 2 A toolbar panel opens beyond the notice stack, on its trigger's side, and an open panel, the Running agents list included, paints above docked chat (SURF-1, SURF-2, CC-1).
  3. 3 Docked chat docks beyond the notice stack on its own edge and never covers the bar's row; with chat expanded, Disable Reloads stays visible and its popover opens without collapsing or moving chat (SURF-1, SURF-6, O2).
  4. 4 The Configuration window's height is capped by the dock's inset, so every notice's text stays readable beside it while the window holds Tab and makes the notices inert (SURF-4, KEY-7).
  5. 5 While a library's devtools panel is docked at the bottom edge, the dock sits on its top edge, or at the top of the window when the panel is taller than half of it, and follows its resize handle; it returns to the person's placement when the panel closes, and the move is never saved (SURF-1, SURF-7).
  6. 6 Along the right edge, from the window edge inward: the minimap strip, the pinned log panel while it is open, then the dock and docked chat. While a page element under the dock or chat has focus, both step aside to the other end of their edge and come back when focus leaves, never saved (SURF-5, SURF-9).
  7. 7 When the surfaces beside the dock cannot keep their header, a first row and their actions in view, the stack shows its first notice and folds the rest into "N more", which expands in place in the same order (FB-8, SURF-9).

5. Type into a devhost field without the page seeing the keys

Text entry stays in devhost's Shadow DOM, and devhost's own handlers, React's and CodeMirror's included, still get every key typed into its fields. What changes: the page's capture-phase listeners no longer see those keys, and a click on the toolbar no longer takes the page's keyboard away. This replaces today's documented "Click the page to give it the keyboard back." (CC-28).

  1. 1 On a page whose '/' shortcut listens in the capture phase, typing feature/login into Find worktree keeps all 13 characters in the field with focus there; the page's listener receives none of their keydown, keypress, keyup or beforeinput events. The same holds in the chat composer, the configuration text, filter fields, the Disable Reloads request, the Deny note and the crop fields (KEY-5).
  2. 2 A small classic script, first in <head>, registers window capture listeners before any page script. For an event aimed at a devhost text field it stops the original, dispatches a clone inside the shadow root and copies the clone's preventDefault() onto the original, so typing proceeds (KEY-5).
  3. 3 A focused bar item or notice action keeps only Enter, Space, the arrow keys, Home, End and Escape; every other key reaches the page. Inside an open panel, menu or modal dialog devhost keeps every key. A panel opened with the pointer (a mouse, pen or touch pointerdown on its trigger) returns focus, on Escape or a click outside, to the page element that had it; one opened from the keyboard, or by an assistive technology's click, returns focus to its trigger (KEY-5, KEY-6, KEY-7).

6. Use the page-wide chords

  3. 3 The section names the clashes a person may meet and how to rebind. Whether a person can override the team's bindings from their own devhost config file is O17 (KEY-2, KEY-13).
